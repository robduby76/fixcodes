LG Washer Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every LG washer code explained on FixCodes, 7 in all. Together they cite 31 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
CLChild Lock Is On (How to Turn It Off)
CL on an LG washer isn't a fault.
dE1Door Not Closed or Not Locking
dE1 on an LG front-load washer is a door error: the washer thinks the door is open or not fully closed, so it won't start.
dE2Door Closed but Won't Lock
dE2 on an LG front-load washer means the door is closed but the washer can't lock it, so the cycle won't start.
IEWater Inlet Error, Washer Not Filling
IE on an LG washer is a Water Inlet Error: the tub didn't fill to the right level within the time the washer allows.
LEMotor Locked, Tub Won't Spin
LE on an LG washer is a motor error: the tub isn't spinning properly because the motor can't turn.
OEWasher Won't Drain
OE on an LG washer is a drain error (LG calls it a Water Outlet Error): the washer can't pump the water out.
UEUnbalanced Load
UE on an LG washer means the load is unbalanced and the washer can't reach full spin speed.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
