Daikin Mini Split Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Daikin mini split code explained on FixCodes, 18 in all. Together they cite 46 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
A3Drain Water Level Problem
A3 on a Daikin system is a drain level control system abnormality: the float switch in the indoor unit's drain pan has tripped because condensate isn't draining away.
E1Outdoor Unit PC Board Abnormality
E1 on a Daikin air conditioner or heat pump points at the outdoor unit's control board.
E2Ground Leakage Detected
E2 on a Daikin VRV outdoor unit means the outdoor unit's main board detected electrical current leaking to ground (an earth fault), or that the ground leakage detection…
E3High Pressure Switch Tripped
E3 on a Daikin air conditioner or heat pump means the outdoor unit's high pressure switch opened, so the system shut down to protect itself from excessive refrigerant…
E4Low Pressure Trip
E4 on a Daikin air conditioner or heat pump means the outdoor unit saw refrigerant suction (low-side) pressure drop too low, so it shut down to protect the compressor.
E5Compressor Overload or Compressor Motor Lock
E5 on a Daikin air conditioner or heat pump is a compressor protection code.
E6Compressor Lock or Start-Up Abnormality
E6 on a Daikin air conditioner or heat pump means the compressor couldn't start or locked up.
E7Outdoor Fan Motor Lock
E7 on a Daikin mini split means the outdoor DC fan motor is locked or not turning at the speed the board expects.
E9Electronic Expansion Valve Coil Fault

F3Discharge Pipe Temperature Too High
F3 (shown as F3-00 on newer remotes) on a Daikin mini split means the outdoor unit's discharge pipe, the hot gas line leaving the compressor, got too hot, or the sensor…
L5Inverter Output Overcurrent
L5 (shown as L5-00 on newer remotes) on a Daikin mini split means the outdoor unit's inverter board saw a sudden current spike going to the compressor.
U0Refrigerant Shortage (Insufficient Gas)
U0 on a Daikin system means the unit thinks it's short of refrigerant (Daikin's manuals call it "Insufficient Gas" or "Refrigerant shortage").
U2Power Supply Voltage Problem

U3Check Operation Not Executed
U3 on a Daikin SkyAir or VRV system means the unit was started without its required check operation (the test run) being completed.
U4Indoor and Outdoor Units Can't Communicate
U4 on a Daikin system is a transmission (communication) error between the indoor and outdoor units.

UHSystem Address Undefined
UH on a Daikin VRV system means the system found an indoor unit that never got its refrigerant-system address during automatic addressing.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
