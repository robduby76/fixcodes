Samsung Oven C-A2 Error Code: Cooling Motor Operating Abnormally (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-10-23 · Based on 4 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

C-A2 on a Samsung wall oven means the cooling motor (the fan that keeps the controls and electronics cool) is operating abnormally. Samsung's owner's manuals give one fix for it: call for service. Its service manual starts with the wiring between the main control board (connector CN202) and the cooling motor, then checks whether the motor itself runs. Don't keep baking with the code showing.

What the code means

Samsung's built-in wall oven owner's manuals (for example the NV51T5511 series) list C-A2 as "The cooling motor is operating abnormally" with the solution "Call for service." Dacor, Samsung's premium brand, lists the same code on its combination wall oven as "Cooling motor is abnormally operated." The cooling motor drives the fan that pulls air through the cabinet and around the control area so the electronics don't overheat; it is not the convection fan inside the oven cavity. Unlike codes such as C-21 or C-30, Samsung gives no owner reset step for C-A2. Samsung's NV51K7770D service manual tells technicians to check that the wire between the wall main PCB (connector CN202) and the motor connector is correctly connected, reconnect it if not, and then check the cooling motor's operation. Codes can vary between models, so check the information-code table in your own manual.

Likely causes

  • A loose or disconnected cooling motor connector at the motor end
  • A loose or poorly seated connector at the wall main control board (CN202 on the NV51K7770D series)
  • A damaged or pinched wire in the harness between the board and the motor
  • A cooling motor that has failed, is seized, or isn't turning at the expected speed
  • A main control board fault, if the motor and wiring check out

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before opening panels.

  1. Turn the oven off and note exactly what the display shows (C-A2) and when it appeared, for example during a bake or self-clean.
  2. Samsung's owner's manual says to call for service for C-A2, and there's no owner reset listed for it. Don't keep cooking with the code showing, since the cooling fan protects the controls from heat.
  3. If you want to rule out a one-off glitch, switch the oven's circuit breaker off for at least 30 seconds and back on (Samsung's general step for its other information codes). If C-A2 comes back, stop and book service.
  4. Write down the model and serial number from the label (usually visible when the oven door is open) before you call, so the technician can bring the right cooling motor.
  5. Leave the connector and motor checks to a technician. On a built-in wall oven they mean disconnecting power, pulling the oven out of the cabinet and opening the back cover, and the oven runs on 240 V.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • Any time C-A2 shows. Samsung's only listed solution is to call for service.
  • C-A2 returns after a power reset.
  • You hear the cooling fan grinding, rattling or not running at all after the oven has been on, or the control panel area feels unusually hot.
  • The oven is under warranty. Samsung's US warranty service goes through a Samsung authorized service center.
