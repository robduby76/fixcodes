York Furnace Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every York furnace code explained on FixCodes, 7 in all. Together they cite 6 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Seven red flashes on a York furnace control board mean the furnace tried to light three times and never established a flame, so it locked out.

9 red flashesReversed Polarity, Grounding Problem or Reversed Transformer Wires
Nine red flashes on a York furnace control board mean the control sees reversed line voltage polarity, a grounding problem, or reversed low-voltage transformer wires.
11 red flashesMain Limit Open Over 5 Minutes, Likely Blower Failure
Eleven red flashes on a York furnace control board mean the main limit switch opened and stayed open for more than 5 minutes.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
