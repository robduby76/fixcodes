Code: E31🧺 Bosch Dishwasher

Bosch Dishwasher E31: Zeolith Drying Heater Fault or E31-00 Leak Protection (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-10-25 · Based on 6 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

E31 on a Bosch dishwasher can mean two different things, so check exactly what the display shows. A plain E31 on a Zeolith (PerfectDry / CrystalDry) model points to a fault with the heater in the zeolite drying system; Bosch says to unplug the dishwasher to reset it and call service if it comes back. E31-00 or E3100 on newer models is Bosch's four-digit version of E15: water has been detected in the base and the leak protection system has switched on, so turn off the water supply first.

What the code means

Bosch France describes error E31 as a problem with the zeolite heating element (résistance zéolithe) on dishwashers with Zeolith drying, which Bosch sells as PerfectDry in Europe and CrystalDry in the US. Zeolite is a mineral held in a tank in the bottom of the dishwasher that absorbs moisture during washing and releases it as heat to dry the dishes; the system is sealed and the zeolite itself never needs replacing. According to Bosch, the code is triggered by moisture in the zeolite tank or by a dirty water sensor, the dishwasher then runs without that heater, and if the code persists after a reset the zeolite heater most likely needs to be replaced. Newer Bosch dishwashers show four-digit codes instead: Bosch USA lists E3100 as the safety switch detecting water in the base of the dishwasher, with the leakage protection system activated, and Bosch Austria and France list E3100 and E31-00 together with E15. That E31-00 code has nothing to do with the Zeolith dryer.

Likely causes

  • Plain E31 (Zeolith models): moisture getting into the zeolite tank in the base of the dishwasher (per Bosch France).
  • Plain E31 (Zeolith models): a dirty or fouled water sensor (per Bosch France).
  • Plain E31 (Zeolith models): a failed zeolite heating element, which Bosch says is the most likely repair when the code won't clear.
  • E31-00 / E3100: water in the base tray from a leak, which trips the float safety switch (the same fault as E15).
  • E31-00 / E3100: a leaking door seal, or a kinked, blocked or cracked drain hose (Bosch France lists both).
  • E31-00 / E3100: a damaged pressure switch or heat exchanger inside the dishwasher (Bosch France says to leave this to a technician).

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the dishwasher or switch off its breaker before removing any panels, and turn off the water supply for any leak code. Don't tilt a dishwasher with water in the base. Zeolite heater and internal leak repairs are best left to a Bosch-trained technician.

  1. Read the display carefully. A plain E31 is the Zeolith dryer code; E31-00 or E3100 is the leak protection code. The fixes are completely different.
  2. For a plain E31: turn the dishwasher off and unplug it or switch off its breaker for a few minutes, then restore power. Bosch says this can be enough to reset it. If E31 comes back, book a Bosch service visit, since the zeolite heater sits sealed in the base and usually needs replacing.
  3. For E31-00 or E3100: turn off the water supply to the dishwasher and switch it off. Bosch advises against trying to repair a leak protection fault yourself.
  4. For E31-00 or E3100: look around the base of the dishwasher and along the hoses for leaks, kinks or damage, and check the door seals. A dried leak usually leaves a white limescale trace that shows where the water came from.
  5. Don't tilt the dishwasher to drain water from the base; Bosch warns this risks electric shock or injury. You can bail out standing water in the tub while you wait for service.
  6. If a hose is visibly damaged, replace it with the correct Bosch part for your model; otherwise call Bosch service (US: 1-800-944-2904).

🛠 When to call a pro

  • Confirm the model has Zeolith drying (PerfectDry / CrystalDry) before treating E31 as a drying-system fault.
  • For E31 on a Zeolith model, check the zeolite tank for moisture, clean or check the water sensor, and test the zeolite heating element and its wiring; replace the heater if it is open or the fault returns.
  • For E31-00 / E3100, find the source of the water in the base tray (door seals, hoses, pump, heat exchanger, pressure switch), repair it, dry the base and check the float switch moves freely before reset.
  • Run a test cycle and watch for the code to return before closing the job.
