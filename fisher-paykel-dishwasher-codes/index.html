Fisher & Paykel Dishwasher Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Fisher & Paykel dishwasher code explained on FixCodes, 9 in all. Together they cite 19 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
A1Water Supply Fault on a DishDrawer
A1 on a Fisher & Paykel DishDrawer means a water supply fault: the drawer couldn't get enough water in to start or carry on the wash.
A7Excess Foam, Auto Recovery Running
A7 on a Fisher & Paykel DishDrawer means the drawer has detected excess foam and has started its own auto recovery program to clear it.
E1Flood Detected
E1 on a Fisher & Paykel DishDrawer means "Flood detected": the flood sensor in the base of the cabinet has picked up water, so the dishwasher stops washing and runs the…
E6Drying Fan Fault or Flood Switch
E6 means two different things depending on which Fisher & Paykel dishwasher you have.
E20Drain Fault, Water Not Draining
E20 (shown as A20 on many models) on a Fisher & Paykel full-size dishwasher means a drain fault: the dishwasher didn't drain enough water out of the tub.
E30Flood Switch Activated, Water in the Base
E30 (shown as F30 on many models) on a Fisher & Paykel full-size dishwasher means the flood switch in the base pan has detected water, so the dishwasher stops and beeps.
F1Flood Switch Activated
F1 on a Fisher & Paykel DishDrawer is a flood fault: the flood switch in the base of the dishwasher has detected water where it shouldn't be.
F2Motor Not Turning, DishDrawer Motor Fault
F2 on a Fisher & Paykel DishDrawer means a motor fault: the controller hasn't sensed the wash motor turning.
U6Spray Arm Loose or Water Pressure Too Low
U6 on a Fisher & Paykel DishDrawer means the spray arm has loosened or come off its mounting, or the water pressure coming into the drawer is too low.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
