Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
F11Drainage Fault, Water Left in the Tub
F11 on a Miele dishwasher is a drainage fault: water is still sitting in the wash cabinet because it isn't pumping away.
F12Water Inlet Blocked or Restricted
F12 on a Miele dishwasher is a water inlet fault: the machine isn't getting enough water at the start of a fill.
F18Water Intake Valve Fault
F18 on a Miele dishwasher means the water intake valve is faulty: the machine has detected water flowing in when it never asked the valve to open.
F24Heating Relay Contact Fault
F24 on a Miele dishwasher is a heating relay contact fault: the control has found power reaching the heating circuit when it shouldn't.

F78 on a Miele dishwasher means a fault in the circulation pump, the pump that pushes water through the spray arms.
