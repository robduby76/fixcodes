Hotpoint Washer Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Hotpoint washer code explained on FixCodes, 18 in all. Together they cite 31 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
E15Water Will Not Pump Out
E15 on an older Hotpoint washer dryer or washing machine (the models with an Infobubble display, such as the WD72 and WDM73) means the water will not pump out.
F01Control Board (Motor Triac) Fault
F01 on a Hotpoint washing machine is an electronic circuit board fault: the machine won't run, or some or all functions can't be selected.
F02Motor Circuit Fault (Drum Won't Turn or Turns On and Off)
F02 on a Hotpoint washing machine is a motor circuit fault.
F03Temperature Sensing Fault (Wash NTC Sensor)
F03 on a Hotpoint washing machine is a temperature sensing fault: the machine can't get a sensible reading from the wash temperature sensor (an NTC thermistor), so it…
F05Won't Drain (Blocked Pump Filter, Hose or Pump)
F05 on a Hotpoint washing machine means it can't pump the water out.
F06Door Lock Fault (Door Won't Lock, Open or Start)
F06 on a Hotpoint washing machine or washer dryer (the UK and European Hotpoint made by Whirlpool EMEA) means a door lock fault.
F08Heater Fault (Programme Pauses Mid-Wash)
F08 on a Hotpoint washing machine or washer dryer (the UK and European Hotpoint made by Whirlpool EMEA) means a heater fault.
F09Software Fault (Flashing Lights, Can't Select a Programme)
F09 on a Hotpoint washing machine (the UK and European Hotpoint made by Whirlpool EMEA, formerly Indesit Company) is a software fault.
F9 E1Not Draining (Pump Filter, Hose or Drain Blocked)
F9 E1 (printed F9E1 in the manuals) on a newer Hotpoint washing machine means it isn't draining: the water can't get out of the drum.
F10Pressure Switch Not Sensing Water Level
F10 on a Hotpoint washing machine means the control board isn't getting a full or empty signal from the pressure switch, the part that senses the water level in the drum.
F11Drain Pump Circuit Fault (Water Left in the Drum)
F11 on a Hotpoint washing machine or washer dryer is a pump circuit fault: the machine can't run its drain pump, so water stays in the drum and it won't pump out.
F12Electronic Control Fault (Machine Won't Turn On)
F12 on a Hotpoint washing machine or washer dryer is an electronic control fault: the machine isn't operating or won't turn on properly.
F13Dryer Temperature Sensor Fault
F13 on a Hotpoint washer dryer is a dryer temperature sensing fault: the machine won't dry, or stops during the drying part of the cycle.
F16Drum Lock Position Fault (Top Loaders)
F16 on a Hotpoint washing machine is a drum lock position sensor fault, and it only applies to top-loading machines: the drum has stopped in the wrong position or isn't…
F17Door Lock Not Powered or Door Not Closed
F17 on a Hotpoint (or Hotpoint-Ariston) washing machine is a door lock fault: the electronics either can't see the door as shut or the door lock isn't getting power.
F18Internal Data Error (Display or Buttons Not Working)
F18 on a Hotpoint washing machine or washer dryer is an internal data error: the electronics on the main control board have hit a fault, so some or all of the display…
F19Fan Motor or Heating Fault
F19 on a Hotpoint washer dryer is a fan motor or heating fault in the drying system: the machine isn't drying, or it stops part way through the drying cycle.
H20Not Filling With Water (Tap, Hose, Pressure or Inlet Filter)
H20 on a Hotpoint washing machine is a pun on H2O: the machine has failed to take in water.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
