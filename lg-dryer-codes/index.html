LG Dryer Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every LG dryer code explained on FixCodes, 7 in all. Together they cite 15 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
9A5It Actually Says "GAS" (No Gas Supply)
If your LG gas dryer shows what looks like "9A5," you're reading the word GAS on the display.
CLChild Lock Is On (How to Turn It Off)
CL on an LG dryer isn't a fault.
D80Vent About 80% Blocked
d80 on an LG dryer is a Flow Sense warning: the dryer has detected that the exhaust vent is roughly 80% restricted.
D90Vent About 90% Blocked
d90 on an LG dryer is a Flow Sense warning that the exhaust vent is roughly 90% restricted.
D95Vent About 95% Blocked
d95 is the most severe LG Flow Sense warning: the dryer's exhaust vent is about 95% clogged.
nPNo Current Detected at the Heater
nP on an LG electric dryer means the control isn't detecting any current at the heater, and LG says that normally points to the home's electrical supply rather than the…
tE1Thermistor Problem
tE1 on an LG dryer means the control detected a problem with the dryer's thermistor, the temperature sensor that tells the board how hot the air is.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
