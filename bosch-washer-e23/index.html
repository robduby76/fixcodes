Code: E23🧺 Bosch Washer

Bosch Washer E23 (F23, E00-23) Error Code: AquaStop Activated, Water in the Base (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-10-29 · Based on 6 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

E23 (also shown as F23, F:23 or E00-23) on a Bosch washing machine means the AquaStop leak protection has been triggered: there is water in the base tub of the machine or the machine is leaking. Turn off the water tap straight away and don't use the washer until the leak has been found and repaired. Bosch says this fault can't be cleared by the owner and needs a service visit, but you can safely check for obvious outside causes, such as a loose hose, a door seal problem or too much foam, while you wait.

What the code means

Bosch washing machines with AquaStop have a tray in the base of the cabinet that catches any water leaking inside the machine. When water collects there, the machine shows E23 (F23 or E00-23 depending on the display) and the AquaStop system shuts off the water supply. Bosch US describes the code as the AquaStop hose being activated because of a possible leak, and says not to use the washer until it has been fixed. A Bosch owner's manual puts it plainly: F:23 means "water in the base tub, appliance leaking. Call the after-sales service." Bosch Australia and South Africa both say the fault "can't be rectified by itself" and tell owners to book a service appointment. Bosch Singapore adds that the place where water shows up is a good clue to the cause: at the front usually points to the door, detergent drawer or filter area, at the back to the fill or drain hose, and underneath could be any of these. Because the water has to drain from the base tray and the leak inside the machine has to be found, E23 is normally a technician job even after you've fixed an outside cause.

Likely causes

  • A leak inside the machine that drips into the base tray, for example from an internal hose, the pump or the tub (Bosch: water in the base tub, appliance leaking)
  • Fill hose or drain hose loose or not fully connected at the back of the machine or at the tap or waste outlet (Bosch Singapore)
  • Door not shut properly, laundry caught in the door, or a dirty or damaged rubber door seal (Bosch Singapore)
  • Leaks around the detergent drawer or the pump filter cap at the bottom front (Bosch Singapore)
  • Too much foam from overdosing detergent, especially with soft water or lightly soiled laundry (Bosch Singapore)
  • Drum overfilled or the machine not standing level (Bosch Singapore)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Turn off the water tap immediately, as Bosch advises, then switch the machine off and unplug it before you look for the leak.
  2. Mop up any water on the floor and note where it appears: front, back or underneath. Bosch says the location is usually a good indication of the cause.
  3. Check the fill hose at the tap and at the back of the machine, and the drain hose at the machine and the waste outlet, for loose connections or cracks. Don't put the AquaStop safety box on the hose in water: Bosch warns it contains an electrical valve.
  4. Check that the pump filter cap at the bottom front is screwed in fully and the detergent drawer is pushed all the way in.
  5. Clean the rubber door seal and the door glass so no lint or detergent residue sits where they meet, and check nothing is trapped in the door. Close the door until it clicks.
  6. For future washes, dose detergent as the packet says for your water hardness and soil level, don't overfill the drum, and make sure the machine stands level.
  7. Then arrange a service visit. Bosch says the E23 fault can't be cleared by the owner, and there is water in the base of the machine that has to be dealt with. Bosch Customer Support in the US is 1-800-944-2904.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • Any time E23 appears: Bosch's instruction for this code is to contact customer service and book a repair, and not to use the washer until it's fixed.
  • Water keeps appearing underneath the machine even though the hoses, filter cap, door and drawer look fine, since that suggests a leak inside the cabinet.
  • The AquaStop hose itself is cracked, kinked or damaged. It's a Bosch safety part with an electrical valve inside, so have it replaced rather than repaired.
  • The machine is under warranty, because opening the cabinet yourself can affect cover.
