Maytag Oven Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Maytag oven code explained on FixCodes, 7 in all. Together they cite 31 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
F1Control, Oven Sensor or Wiring Fault
F1 on a Maytag range or oven means the control has flagged a fault in its own circuitry or in something it monitors: Maytag says there may be a problem with the oven…
F1 E0Oven Control Memory (EEPROM) Error
F1 E0 (F1E0) on a Maytag range or wall oven is a fault inside the electronic oven control.
F1 E1Oven Control Board Fault
F1 E1 (F1E1) on a Maytag range or wall oven points to the electronic oven control, not to a heating element or the door.
F3Oven Temperature Sensor Circuit
F3 on a Maytag range or oven points to the oven temperature sensor circuit: the control is reading the sensor as open or shorted, or not getting a sensible reading at…
F5 E1Door Latch Not Working
F5 E1 (F5E1) on a Maytag range or wall oven means the self-clean door latch isn't working the way the control expects.
F6 E1Over Temperature or Control Communication Fault
F6 E1 (F6E1) on a Maytag range or wall oven is a control-system fault.
F9Door Lock Fault or Miswired Power
F9 on a Maytag oven means one of two things, depending on the model.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
