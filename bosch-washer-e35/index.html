Code: E35🧺 Bosch Washer

Bosch Washer E35 (E:35-10) Error Code: Water in the Base, Machine Is Leaking (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-11-02 · Based on 5 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

On newer Bosch washing machines, E:35 / -10 means there is water in the base trough of the machine, in other words the appliance is leaking inside. It is the newer-style version of the old E23 / F23 AquaStop code. Bosch's instruction for owners is short: turn off the water tap and call Bosch service. You can mop up, look for an obvious outside cause and keep the machine off until it has been checked, but Bosch says this fault can't be cleared by the owner.

What the code means

Bosch's newer washers show faults as a main code plus a sub-code, and E:35 / -10 is the leak code. In Bosch owner's manuals for current models (for example WGB244A0BY and WIW24342EU), the troubleshooting table lists E:35 / -10 with two causes: "There is water in the base trough" and "The appliance is leaking", and the fix for both is the same: turn off the water tap and call the after-sales service. The base trough is a tray in the bottom of the cabinet that catches water leaking inside the machine; on models with AquaStop, water there triggers the AquaStop system, which shuts off the water supply. Bosch France and Bosch Netherlands group E35-10 with the older E23 and F23 codes under the heading "AquaStop activated" and say the owner can't fix it, so book a technician. Bosch US describes the same fault as "water in the base and/or the appliance is leaking" and says to turn off the tap and call Bosch Customer Support. Because the water has to be drained from the base and the leak inside the machine has to be found and repaired, E:35 / -10 is normally a service job even if you find and fix an outside cause first. If your machine shows the older E23 or F23 instead, see our Bosch E23 guide, which covers the same fault.

Likely causes

  • Water has collected in the base trough of the machine (Bosch manuals WGB244A0BY and WIW24342EU)
  • The appliance is leaking inside the cabinet, for example from a hose, the drum seal area or a pump connection (Bosch manuals list "the appliance is leaking"; the exact leak point needs a technician to find)
  • AquaStop leak protection activated, the same fault as the older E23 / F23 codes (Bosch France, Bosch Netherlands)
  • Contributing outside causes Bosch's general troubleshooting mentions for leaks: a loose or damaged inlet or outlet hose, and dirt on the door or seal causing leaks below the door

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Turn off the water tap first, as Bosch instructs for E:35 / -10. Then switch the machine off and unplug it before you look around it.
  2. Mop up any water on the floor and note where it appears: front, back or underneath. That helps the technician find the leak faster.
  3. Check the outside connections. Bosch's troubleshooting table says water at the inlet hose means the hose isn't connected correctly or securely (reconnect it and tighten the screw connection), and water at the outlet hose means it's damaged or not connected correctly. Don't put an AquaStop safety box in water, since it contains an electric valve.
  4. If water is coming from below the door, Bosch says dirt on the door or seal can cause leaks: clean the door glass and the rubber seal and make sure nothing is caught in the door.
  5. Don't keep running wash cycles to see if the code clears. Bosch's instruction for this code is to call the after-sales service, and running the machine with water in the base can make the leak and any floor damage worse.
  6. Book a service visit and give the exact code shown (E:35 / -10). Bosch Customer Support in the US is 1-800-944-2904.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • Every E:35 / -10. Bosch's manuals and its France and Netherlands support pages say to call the after-sales service, and the owner can't clear the fault.
  • You can see water coming from inside the cabinet, or there's no obvious outside cause such as a loose hose.
  • The machine is under warranty, or your model has the Bosch Aqua-Stop guarantee, which the manual says applies when the system was installed according to Bosch's instructions. Let Bosch service inspect it before anyone else opens it.
