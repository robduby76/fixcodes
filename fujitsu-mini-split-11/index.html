Fujitsu Mini Split Error Code 11: Serial Communication Error (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-10-24 · Based on 3 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

Error 11 (E:11) on a Fujitsu (Halcyon, Airstage) mini split is a serial communication error: the indoor and outdoor units aren't talking to each other over the interconnect wiring. It's most often a wiring or power problem, such as a loose, miswired or spliced connection between the units or low supply voltage, but it can also come from electrical noise, a failed main board or a failed outdoor fan motor.

What the code means

Fujitsu's service manuals list E:11 as "Serial communication error" and split it into two cases. In a serial reverse transfer error (outdoor unit), the indoor unit can't receive the serial signal from the outdoor unit for more than 2 minutes after power on, or for more than 15 seconds during normal operation; Fujitsu's listed causes are a connection failure, an external cause, a main PCB failure or an outdoor unit fan motor failure. In a serial forward transfer error (indoor unit), the outdoor unit can't properly receive the serial signal from the indoor unit for 10 seconds or more; causes are a connection failure, an external cause or a main PCB failure. On units without a wired remote, the indoor unit shows it as 1 Operation light flash and 1 Timer light flash, with the Economy light flashing continuously. Fujitsu's 410A mini-split troubleshooting guide describes it as the evaporator (indoor) and condenser (outdoor) not communicating at start-up, and says wire nuts and splices in the interconnect are not recommended.

Likely causes

  • A loose, disconnected or miswired interconnect wire between the indoor and outdoor units, or a loose connector or open cable inside the control box.
  • Wire nuts or splices in the indoor-to-outdoor cable, which Fujitsu's troubleshooting guide says are not recommended.
  • Supply voltage at the outdoor unit outside Fujitsu's range (AC 103.5 to 126.5 V on 115 V models; AC 198 to 264 V on 220-240 V models).
  • External electrical noise, such as a poor ground or equipment near the power cable that puts out harmonic interference (Fujitsu names neon lights and some electronics).
  • A failed outdoor unit main PCB or outdoor fan motor (reverse transfer error), or a failed indoor unit main PCB (forward transfer error).

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

The E:11 checks are live voltage measurements at the indoor-to-outdoor terminals. Leave terminal testing, rewiring and board or fan motor replacement to a licensed HVAC technician, and switch off power at the breaker and outdoor disconnect before touching any wiring.

  1. Note the light pattern: 1 Operation flash, 1 Timer flash and a continuously flashing Economy light means E:11.
  2. Turn the system off at the breaker or disconnect for a few minutes, then back on, and run it. Fujitsu's first step is a power reset. If the code doesn't come back, the likely cause is electrical noise or a poor ground.
  3. Check whether anything changed recently near the outdoor unit or the line set, such as new wiring, a damaged cable, landscaping work or chewed wires, and whether any new equipment like neon lighting was added near the power cable.
  4. Make sure the breaker for the system isn't tripping or loose and that the outdoor disconnect is fully on.
  5. If the code returns after a reset, call the installer or a licensed HVAC technician. The remaining checks are live voltage tests at the outdoor terminals.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • Interconnect wiring has loose, open, spliced or crossed conductors. Fujitsu says to correct it per the installation manual.
  • Outdoor L-N supply voltage is out of range. Fujitsu's next check is the power supply before any board is condemned.
  • Reverse transfer signal at outdoor terminals 1-3 doesn't swing as specified (AC 90 to 270 V in the cited manuals). Fujitsu says to check the outdoor fan motor; if it's bad, replace the fan motor and outdoor main PCB, and if it's good, replace the outdoor main PCB.
  • Forward transfer signal at outdoor terminals 2-3 doesn't swing between AC 30 and 130 V. Fujitsu says to replace the indoor unit main PCB.
  • The system is new or under warranty. Contact the installer or Fujitsu General America technical support (866-952-8324) first.
