Haier Washer Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Haier washer code explained on FixCodes, 5 in all. Together they cite 19 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
E1Won't Drain or Drains Too Slowly
On a Haier washer, E1 is a drain error: the water didn't empty out, or emptied too slowly.
E2Lid or Door Not Closed or Locked
On a Haier washer, E2 is a lid or door problem.
E4Not Filling With Water in Time (Tap, Pressure, Inlet Filter, Drain Hose)
E4 on a Haier washer means a water inlet timeout: the machine didn't fill to the level it needed within the time allowed.
FCControl Board Can't Talk to the Motor Driver
FC on a Haier washer is an internal communication error: the main control board isn't getting a proper signal back from the motor driver (inverter) board.
UNBUnbalanced Load, Won't Spin Fast
On a Haier washer, UNB (shown as Unb) means the load is unbalanced.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
