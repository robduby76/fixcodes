Frigidaire Washer Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Frigidaire washer code explained on FixCodes, 8 in all. Together they cite 23 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
E1Long Fill Time, Water Not Entering
E1 on a Frigidaire top-load washer means water isn't entering the washer or it's filling too slowly.
E2Water Takes Too Long to Drain
E2 on a Frigidaire top-load washer means water is taking too long to drain or isn't draining properly.
E3Lid Open or Lid Lock Failed
E3 (or just 3) on a Frigidaire top-load washer means the lid is open or the lid lock failed.
E4Load Out of Balance During Spin
E4 (or just 4) on a Frigidaire top-load washer means the load is out of balance during the spin.
E5Washer Is Not Level
E5 on a Frigidaire top-load washer means the washer is not level.
E11Fill Time Too Long
E11 on a Frigidaire front-load washer means the fill took too long: the washer didn't get enough water in the time it expected during the wash phase.
E21Water Not Pumping Out Fast Enough
E21 on a Frigidaire front-load washer means the water isn't pumping out fast enough: the drain took longer than the washer allows.
E41Door Open or Door Switch Problem
E41 on a Frigidaire front-load washer means the control thinks the door is open: it isn't getting a closed signal from the door switch.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
