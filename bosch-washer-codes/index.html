Bosch Washer Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Bosch washer code explained on FixCodes, 10 in all. Together they cite 36 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
E1212) Error Code: Water Not Coming In
E12 (shown as E:12, with the Spin light on) on an older Bosch front-load washer, such as the Nexxt and Vision models, means water isn't getting into the machine, or…
E1313) Error Code: Drain Pump Time Exceeded
E13 (shown as E:13) on an older Bosch front-load washer, such as the Vision and Nexxt models, means the washer didn't drain in the time it expected.
E17Water Supply Time Exceeded
E17 (also shown as F17) on a Bosch washer means the machine couldn't fill with water in the time it expected.
E1818) Error Code: Water Not Draining
E18 (also shown as F18 or E:18) on a Bosch washer means the machine couldn't drain the water: usually the drain hose is kinked or blocked, or the drain pump is clogged…
E2121) Error Code: Motor Fault, Drum Not Turning
On Bosch washing machines, E21 / F21 (printed F:21 in Bosch manuals) is a motor fault: the machine tried to turn the drum and the motor didn't run the way the control…
E23AquaStop Activated, Water in the Base
E23 (also shown as F23, F:23 or E00-23) on a Bosch washing machine means the AquaStop leak protection has been triggered: there is water in the base tub of the machine…
E29Not Enough Water Coming In
E29 (also shown as F29 or E00-29) on a Bosch washer means too little water is reaching the machine.
E3030-10, E:30-20, E:30-80) Error Code: Water Supply, Too Much Foam or Not Draining
On newer Bosch washing machines, E30 always comes with a second number, and that number tells you what's wrong.
E3535-10) Error Code: Water in the Base, Machine Is Leaking
On newer Bosch washing machines, E:35 / -10 means there is water in the base trough of the machine, in other words the appliance is leaking inside.
E3636-10, E:36-25, E:36-26) Error Code: Water Isn't Draining
E36 on a Bosch washer (shown as E:36-10, E:36-25 or E:36-26) points to a drain problem.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
