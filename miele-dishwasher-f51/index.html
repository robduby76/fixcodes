Code: F51🧺 Miele Dishwasher

Miele Dishwasher F51: Pump Pressure Level Switch Fault (Meaning, Causes, What to Do)

Reviewed 2026-10-26 · Based on 5 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

F51 on a Miele dishwasher means a fault with the heater pressure switch, the switch that confirms the circulation pump is building water pressure before the heater is allowed on. Miele calls it a "pump pressure level switch fault" and says it's a technical fault you can't fix yourself, so book a repair. The dishwasher interrupts the program and pumps the water out. The usual culprits are the heater relay, the pressure switch itself, or the wiring between them and the electronics.

What the code means

Miele's support pages in the US, Canada, UK and Australia all describe F51 as "Pump pressure level switch fault" and add: "This is a technical fault that you cannot remedy yourself." Miele's technical manuals explain the check behind it. The heater pressure switch (B1/13) sits in the heater circuit and only closes when the circulation pump is pushing water, so the heater can't switch on in a dry machine. One second after a program starts, the electronics switch on the heater relay and check whether the pressure switch contacts have closed. If no power comes back through that path, F51 is stored, the program is interrupted and the drain pump runs. Because the test runs through both the relay and the switch, F51 can point at either one, or at the wiring between them.

Likely causes

  • A defective heater relay, either with contacts that stay open or a failed coil (the first cause in Miele's G 1 and G 5 technical manuals)
  • An open or loose connection between the heater relay and the electronics (dirty or loose plugs, broken wire)
  • A heater pressure switch that is open- or short-circuited
  • Foreign objects (such as food debris or broken glass) blocking the heater pressure switch

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

F51 involves the heater circuit and mains wiring. Unplug the dishwasher or switch off its breaker before anyone takes panels off, and leave relay, pressure-switch and board work to a technician.

  1. Let the drain pump finish, then switch the dishwasher off.
  2. Make sure the faucet or shutoff valve feeding the dishwasher is fully open, since a machine short on water can't build pump pressure.
  3. Clean the filter combination in the bottom of the tub and check for broken glass or debris around it, then switch the machine on and try a short program.
  4. If F51 comes back, stop there. Miele classes it as a technical fault, and the parts involved sit under the machine and on the electronics.
  5. Note the model and serial number from the rating plate on the edge of the door and book a repair with Miele Service (US 888-996-4353) or a Miele-experienced technician.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • Any repeat F51: Miele's technical manual has the technician check the heater relay first and replace it if its contact stays open or its coil has failed.
  • The relay is fine: check the plug connections and wiring between the relay, the pressure switch and the electronics for continuity.
  • The wiring is fine: confirm the heater pressure switch switches through positions 21/22 and 11/12, remove it to look for blockages, and replace it if it still reads open or has debris inside.
  • The dishwasher is under warranty or extended cover: use Miele Service first.
