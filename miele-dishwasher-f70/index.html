Code: F70🧺 Miele Dishwasher

Miele Dishwasher Error Code F70: Waterproof System Activated (Meaning, Causes, What to Do)

Reviewed 2026-10-24 · Based on 6 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

F70 on a Miele dishwasher means the Waterproof system has reacted: the machine sensed water where it shouldn't be, usually in the sealed pan under the tub, so it cancelled the program and turned on the drain pump. Miele's instructions are to close the water faucet, turn the dishwasher off and unplug it (that's the only way the drain pump stops), and then contact Miele Service. Don't keep restarting it, because F70 means something is leaking.

What the code means

Miele's support pages (USA, Canada, UK and Australia) all describe F70 the same way: "The water protection system has reacted. The current program is canceled and the drain pump switches on." Miele's operating instructions explain what that system is: every Miele dishwasher has a safety valve on the incoming water line and a sealed underside with a water sensor, and if a fault occurs the water intake is stopped and the drain pump is activated. Miele's G 600/G 800 technical information shows the hardware behind it: the inlet hose and valves sit inside a protective outer sleeve, so a leaking valve or hose sends water along that sleeve into the drip tray in the bottom of the machine, where a float switch (B8/3) trips and starts the drain pump. Newer manuals add that the Waterproof system works even when the dishwasher is switched off, as long as it stays plugged in. Older models without a code display signal the same reaction with the flashing Inlet/drain light instead of F70. Don't confuse F70 with F11, a drain fault, or F78, a blocked circulation pump.

Likely causes

  • A leak inside the machine that drips into the sealed base pan, such as a loose or split internal hose or a leaking seal, which lifts the float switch.
  • A leaking water inlet valve or inlet hose, which Miele's design routes through the hose's outer sleeve into the drip tray.
  • Water left in the base pan from an earlier leak, which keeps the float switch up.
  • A float switch or sensor that has failed, though Miele treats any F70 as a fault for its service team to check.

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Turn off the water and unplug the dishwasher before you touch anything; water and line voltage meet in the base of the machine. The inlet connection housing contains an electrical valve, so never cut the hose or submerge that housing. Moving and opening the machine to reach the base pan is a job for Miele Service or a qualified appliance technician.

  1. Close the water faucet or shutoff valve that feeds the dishwasher.
  2. Turn the dishwasher off and unplug it, or switch off its breaker if the plug isn't reachable. The drain pump keeps running until power is removed, which is normal.
  3. Unload the dishes, wipe up any water on the floor and check the outside of the water connection at the faucet for drips. If the screw connection drips, tighten it, as Miele's instructions describe.
  4. Look along the inlet hose for kinks, crushing or splits. Don't cut it or put the connection housing in water: it contains an electrical valve.
  5. Leave the water off and contact Miele Service. Miele lists no user reset for F70, so don't keep restarting the machine to see if it clears.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • Pull the dishwasher out, drain and dry the base pan, and find the leak source before returning it to service.
  • Inspect the internal hoses, sump and pump seals, the inlet valve and the Waterproof hose's outer sleeve, and replace whatever is leaking.
  • Check the base-pan float switch and its wiring, and confirm it drops back once the pan is dry.
  • Replace a damaged Waterproof inlet hose with the correct Miele part, since the hose carries electrical components.
