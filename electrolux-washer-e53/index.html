Code: E53🧺 Electrolux Washer

Electrolux Washer E53 Error Code: Motor Control Voltage or Motor Triac Sensing Fault (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-10-25 · Based on 5 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

E53 on an Electrolux front-load washer is a motor drive electronics fault, not a clogged filter or an unbalanced load. On North American Electrolux front-loaders with a separate motor control board, Electrolux's tech sheet lists E53 as low voltage to the motor control board. On Electrolux washers with the motor triac on the main board (common outside North America), Electrolux service manuals list it as a faulty motor triac sensing circuit. Either way, a power reset and a check of your outlet and supply are the only real DIY steps; if E53 comes back, the wiring between the boards and then a board replacement are the usual fix.

What the code means

Electrolux uses E53 for two related things depending on the electronics in the washer. The tech data sheet for the Electrolux EIFLW55HIW 27-inch front-load washer lists E53 as "Low Voltage to Motor Control Board" and sends the technician to Test 15, which on Electrolux's washer tech sheet is a check of the wiring between the main control board and the motor control board, replacing the motor control board if the wiring is good. Electrolux service manuals for washers with the motor triac on the main board (for example manuals 599 70 56-70, 599 75 58-02 and 599 75 58-72) list E53 as "Motor triac sensing circuit faulty (wrong input voltage to microprocessor)", with the possible fault given only as a faulty main circuit board; the cycle is blocked and the code is cleared with a reset. In both cases the control has stopped the drum motor because it can't trust the voltage or signal in the motor drive circuit. Nearby codes mean different things: E52 is a tachometer (speed sensor) signal problem, E54 is motor relay contacts sticking on triac machines or over speed on motor-control-board machines, and E57 and E58 are high current on the inverter or a motor phase.

Likely causes

  • Low or unstable supply voltage reaching the washer, such as a long or undersized extension cord, a loose outlet or a shared, overloaded circuit (on North American models E53 is low voltage at the motor control board)
  • A loose, unplugged or damaged wire or connector between the main control board and the motor control board, which is what Electrolux's Test 15 checks
  • A failed motor control board, the replacement Electrolux's tech sheet calls for once the wiring checks good
  • On washers with the motor triac on the main board, a faulty main control board, the only possible fault Electrolux's service manuals list for E53
  • Burn marks or damage on the board from an earlier electrical fault, which the service manuals tell technicians to look for

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Always unplug the washer before removing any panel or touching connectors. Electrolux's tech sheet warns that some internal parts are intentionally not grounded and can give a shock during servicing, and board and voltage checks are for a qualified technician.

  1. Press Cancel or turn the washer off, unplug it (or switch off its breaker) for a few minutes, then plug it back in and start a short cycle to see whether E53 comes back.
  2. Make sure the washer is plugged straight into a properly grounded wall outlet, not an extension cord or power strip, so it gets full voltage.
  3. If other appliances share the circuit or the lights dim when the washer runs, try the washer with fewer loads on that circuit. Electrolux's tech sheet sends supply-related motor codes to a check of the incoming power under load.
  4. If the washer was recently moved or worked on, make sure it wasn't pushed back hard enough to pinch or pull its power cord.
  5. Write down the full model and serial number from the label on the door opening before calling for service or ordering a board, since board part numbers differ by model.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • E53 keeps coming back after a reset on a properly wired outlet. Electrolux's tech sheet has a technician check the wiring between the main control board and the motor control board, then replace the motor control board if the wiring is good.
  • Your washer uses the main-board motor triac design. Electrolux's service manuals say to check that all connectors are fully inserted, then replace the main circuit board and run the diagnostic cycle again to look for further alarms.
  • You see burn marks on a board or smell burning. The service manuals send technicians to a separate burnt-board procedure, because whatever damaged the board can damage a new one.
  • The washer also shows E52, E54, E57, E58 or a 5-series code, which point to the motor, its speed sensor or the motor control rather than supply voltage alone.
  • You'd have to remove panels or test live 120 V wiring to reach the boards.
