CodeWhat it means
10Combustion Abnormality Warning
Code 10 on a Noritz tankless water heater means a combustion abnormality.
11Ignition Failure
Code 11 on a Noritz tankless water heater means ignition failure: the heater sees a call for hot water but can't light the burner, or lights it but doesn't detect the…
12Flame Loss
Code 12 on a Noritz tankless water heater means flame loss: the burner lights, but the flame goes out or stays too weak while the heater is running.
14Thermal Fuse Tripped by Overheating
Code 14 on a Noritz tankless water heater means the heat exchanger overheated badly enough to blow the thermal fuse, a one-time safety device that shuts the burner off.
20High Limit Switch Tripped (Overheat)
Error code 20 (EC20) on a Noritz tankless water heater means the high limit switch has tripped because the water in or leaving the heat exchanger got dangerously hot.
29Condensate Drain Problem
Error code 29 on a Noritz tankless water heater means the unit is having trouble draining its condensate.
51Gas Solenoid Valve Failure
Code 51 on a Noritz tankless water heater means the control board found a problem with the gas solenoid valves or the circuit that drives them.
90Combustion Abnormality

99Combustion Abnormality Lockout
Code 99 on a Noritz tankless water heater means a combustion abnormality bad enough that the unit has locked itself out.
