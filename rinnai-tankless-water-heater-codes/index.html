Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
10Air Supply or Exhaust Blockage
Code 10 on a Rinnai tankless water heater means Air Supply or Exhaust Blockage: the heater can't move enough combustion air in or exhaust out, so it shuts down.
11No Ignition, Heater Won't Light
Code 11 on a Rinnai tankless water heater means no ignition: the heater tried to light the burner and never detected a flame, so you get no hot water.
12Flame Failure, No Flame Detected
Code 12 on a Rinnai tankless water heater is a flame failure: the heater isn't seeing a proper flame at the burner, so it shuts down and you lose hot water.
