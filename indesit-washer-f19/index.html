Code: F19🧺 Indesit Washer

Indesit Washer Dryer F19 Error Code: Fan Motor or Heating Fault

Reviewed 2026-10-26 · Based on 4 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

F19 on an Indesit washer dryer is a fan motor or heating fault in the drying system: the machine won't dry, or it stops part way through a drying cycle. It only appears on washer dryers, not plain washing machines, which is why it isn't on Indesit's washing machine code list. Indesit's advice is to book an engineer, because the drying fan, drying heater and their wiring sit inside the machine on mains voltage.

What the code means

Indesit Service UK's washer dryer error code guide lists F19 as "Fan motor or heating fault (washer dryers only)", with the symptom that the washer dryer is not drying or stops during the drying cycle, and its only advice is to call customer service on 0344 822 4224 to book an engineer. Indesit's washing machine guide stops at F18, because a plain washer has no drying fan or drying heater. A washer dryer dries by blowing air with a fan over a separate drying heater, and F19 means the electronics found a problem on the fan side or the heating side of that circuit. Indesit doesn't publish a component-level test for F19; its older WIL10 technical documentation covers the neighbouring drying codes instead (F13 drying sensor open, F14 drying heater open or unplugged, F15 drying heater stuck on), which shows the parts an engineer will look at. Sister brand Hotpoint uses the same F19 wording on its washer dryers. If your machine also won't heat water on a wash, mention that to the engineer, since F15 on the same list is a heater control fault that can affect both washing and drying.

Likely causes

  • A failed or seized drying fan motor, so no air moves over the drying heater (the fan side of Indesit's F19 description)
  • A failed drying heater element or a tripped thermal cut-out on it (the heating side of the description)
  • Loose, burnt or unplugged wiring to the drying fan or drying heater
  • A fault on the main control board that switches the fan or heater

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Note when it happens: only on drying programmes, or on wash-only programmes too? That tells the engineer where to start.
  2. Run a wash-only programme. If it washes normally, you can keep washing and air-drying until the repair.
  3. Switch the machine off and unplug it for a couple of minutes, then try a drying programme again. Indesit gives this reset for some other codes on the same list, not for F19, so treat it as a one-off try.
  4. Keep drying loads within the drying limit in your manual; an overloaded drum dries slowly, though it won't cause or clear a fan or heater fault.
  5. If F19 comes back, stop there and book an engineer: the fan and heater are inside the machine on mains voltage.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • F19 returns after a power reset: Indesit's only listed fix is an engineer visit.
  • You smell burning or see scorching near the top of the machine during drying: unplug it and don't use the drying function until it's checked.
  • The machine also won't heat on wash programmes, which points to a wider heater control or board fault.
  • The washer dryer is under warranty: call Indesit service on 0344 822 4224 rather than opening it up.
