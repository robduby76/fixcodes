Code: F15🧺 Indesit Washer

Indesit Washing Machine F15 Error Code: Heater Control Fault

Reviewed 2026-10-26 · Based on 2 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

F15 on an Indesit washing machine or washer dryer is a heater control fault: the main board has lost proper control of a heating element, so the machine stops heating or pauses mid-cycle (on a washer dryer, it won't dry or pauses while drying). Indesit's service documentation for washer dryers is more specific: the drying heater is stuck on because a relay contact on the board has welded shut or the switching component has shorted. That's a safety shutdown, so Indesit's advice is to book an engineer rather than keep running it.

What the code means

Indesit Service UK's washing machine error code guide lists F15 as "Heater control fault (Washing Machine and Washer Dryers)", with the symptom that the machine is not heating water or pauses during a wash cycle, or on a washer dryer is not drying or pauses during the drying cycle, and the advice to call Indesit to book an engineer. Indesit's technical documentation for the WIL10 washer dryer (the French service file, mod. WIL10FR) gives the engineer's definition: F15 means the drying heater is always active, because the drying relay contact is stuck closed or the drying triac (the electronic switch on the board that turns the heater on and off) has short-circuited. The service steps, in order, are: check the contacts of connector J2 on the main board; check the wiring from connector J2 to the drying heater; check the wiring from connector J3 to the pressure switch; and then replace the main board. In plain terms, the board can see a heater running when it hasn't asked for it, so it stops the cycle to avoid overheating. Because the usual cause is on the board itself, this is rarely a DIY fix. For contrast, the same Indesit guide lists F03 as a temperature sensing fault (not heating), and the WIL10 file lists F13 (drying temperature sensor open) and F14 (drying heater open or unplugged) as separate dryer faults. Indesit doesn't publish a component-level definition of F15 for plain washing machines, so on a washer without a dryer, treat it as the general heater control fault Indesit describes.

Likely causes

  • Drying relay contact on the main board stuck (welded) closed, so the dryer heater stays on (Indesit WIL10 service file)
  • Drying triac on the main board short-circuited (same file)
  • Loose, burnt or corroded contacts at the heater connector on the main board (connector J2 in the WIL10 service file)
  • Damaged wiring between the board and the heater, or between the board and the pressure switch (connector J3 in the same file)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Stop the cycle and switch off at the wall. Don't keep restarting a machine showing F15: the fault means a heater may not be switching off properly.
  2. Unplug it for at least 2 minutes, plug it back in and try a short cold wash. If F15 comes back straight away or as soon as the machine tries to heat or dry, leave it unplugged and book an engineer.
  3. If water or washing is trapped inside, let the machine cool, then drain it through the pump filter at the bottom front (have towels and a shallow tray ready).
  4. Check the socket and plug for scorch marks or a burning smell, and avoid running the machine on an extension lead.
  5. If you're experienced with appliance repairs: with the machine unplugged, you can check that the heater and pressure switch plugs on the main board are fully seated and not discoloured. Testing a triac or relay and replacing the board is model-specific, so stop there and book an engineer, as Indesit advises.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • F15 comes back after a power reset: Indesit recommends booking an engineer.
  • You smell burning, see scorch marks on a connector, or the machine got very hot: unplug it and don't use it until it's been checked.
  • The wiring checks out: Indesit's next step is replacing the main board, which usually has to be matched or programmed for your exact model.
  • The machine is under warranty: contact Indesit service rather than opening it up.
