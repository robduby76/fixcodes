Code: F11🔧 LG Microwave

LG Microwave F11 Error Code: Inverter Communication Error (What to Do)

Reviewed 2026-10-25 · Based on 6 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

F11 (shown as F-11) on an LG inverter microwave, including NeoChef models, means an inverter communication error: the main control board has lost contact with the inverter board that powers the magnetron, so the microwave won't heat. Unplug it for 2 to 3 minutes and plug it back in. If F11 comes back, LG says service is required. The fix is inside the cabinet next to high-voltage parts, so it is a job for a technician, and it isn't always the expensive inverter: LG's service manual checks the wiring harness and connectors first.

What the code means

LG's service manual for the LMC1575 NeoChef countertop models (P/No. MFL69902602, February 2017) lists F-11 in its error code table as "Inverter communication Error", alongside F-3 key error, F-4 humidity sensor error and the inverter fault codes F-13 to F-17 and F-19. Its inverter troubleshooting page shows the symptom as "Not heating (F-11)" and has the technician check the connection between the inverter PCB and the main PCB (connectors CN3 and CN8) for an open or short. The remedy steps are: check the communication harness at the PCB connectors, check the connector pins with a tester for open or short, replace the inverter PCB if the connector is shorted, and check the inverter's IC parts. LG's own help library has an "LG Microwave Oven - F 11 Error Code" article filed under inspection codes F-1 to F-19, and LG's general microwave error-code guidance says these codes indicate a failure mode: unplug the power cord, plug it in again after 2 to 3 minutes, and if the problem isn't solved, service is required. LG's USA help library adds that F codes other than F-3 can indicate defective parts, short circuits, communication failures or overcurrent, so an inspection by a technician is recommended. Appliance Aid's LG NeoChef table gives the same meaning (inverter communication error, a wiring or inverter issue). F11 only applies to inverter models. Appliance Aid lists a different F-11 on some older LG convection combination ovens, where it means the oven stayed above 100 C after the 30-minute maximum in convection mode, so check which kind of microwave you have.

Likely causes

  • A loose, damaged or corroded harness or connector between the main control board and the inverter board (LG's manual checks this first)
  • A failed inverter PCB (LG's manual says to replace it if the communication connector tests shorted)
  • A fault on the main control board side of the link
  • A temporary glitch, for example after a power outage or surge, that clears after a power reset

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before opening panels.

  1. Unplug the microwave (or switch off its breaker) for 2 to 3 minutes, then plug it back in. That is LG's first step for F codes.
  2. Try a short heating test with a cup of water. If F11 doesn't return, it was a temporary error.
  3. Make sure the microwave has its own properly rated outlet and isn't sharing the circuit with another high-draw appliance like a toaster or kettle.
  4. Note whether F11 appeared after a power outage, a surge or a recent repair, and give that to the technician.
  5. Don't open the cabinet. The inverter and its capacitors are high-voltage parts that can hold a dangerous charge after unplugging.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • F11 comes back after a power reset: LG says service is required.
  • The microwave runs (light, fan, turntable) but doesn't heat: the technician will check the inverter-to-main-board harness and connectors (CN3 and CN8 on LMC1575 models) before replacing boards.
  • The harness tests good: the technician checks the inverter PCB and its IC parts, then the main control board.
  • You see other inverter codes such as F-13, F-14, F-15, F-16, F-17 or F-19, or smell burning: unplug it and book service.
