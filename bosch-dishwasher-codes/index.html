Bosch Dishwasher Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Bosch dishwasher code explained on FixCodes, 7 in all. Together they cite 26 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
E07CrystalDry (Zeolite) Drying Fan Fault
E07 on a Bosch dishwasher means the fan for the Zeolite (CrystalDry) drying system is defective.
E09Heater Fault, Dishes Washed Without Heat
E09 on a Bosch dishwasher means the heating circuit has a fault.
E15Water in the Base, Leak Protection Activated
E15 on a Bosch dishwasher means the safety switch in the base has detected water and the leak protection system has switched on.
E22Filter Blocked or Not Seated
E22 on a Bosch dishwasher means the filter is blocked.
E24Not Draining, Drain Hose or Filter Blocked
E24 on a Bosch dishwasher means water isn't draining out properly.
E25Drain Pump Blocked or Pump Cover Loose
E25 on a Bosch dishwasher means the drain pump is blocked or the drain pump cover is loose or missing, so water can't be pumped out.
E31Zeolith Drying Heater Fault or E31-00 Leak Protection
E31 on a Bosch dishwasher can mean two different things, so check exactly what the display shows.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
