Code: 51🚿 Noritz Tankless Water Heater

Noritz Error Code 51: Gas Solenoid Valve Failure (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-10-24 · Based on 2 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

Code 51 on a Noritz tankless water heater means the control board found a problem with the gas solenoid valves or the circuit that drives them. Noritz's service manuals list three causes: a loose or improper valve connection, damaged valve wiring, or a failed circuit board. Turning the power off and back on resets the code, but if 51 keeps coming back, the gas valves and board need a technician.

What the code means

Noritz service manuals list error code 51 as Failure of Gas Solenoid Valve (or Gas Solenoid Valve drive circuit abnormality). The display window on the unit may show 51 or 511, while the remote controller shows 51. The gas solenoid valves open and close to let gas reach the burner: the primary valve (SV0) sits on the gas valve and three more (SV1, SV2 and SV3) sit on the manifold plate. Noritz says the code appears when a gas solenoid valve's connections are improper, when those connections are damaged, or when the circuit board has failed. The error table says that to reset code 51, power has to be disconnected and then reconnected. The diagnostic flow chart has a technician check whether any of the SV0 to SV3 connectors on the manifold plate and gas valve came loose, then measure each valve's coil resistance and inspect the wiring. A bad SV0 means replacing the gas valve, a bad SV1, SV2 or SV3 means replacing the manifold plate, and if the valves and wiring test normal, the circuit board is replaced. Noritz lists a separate code, 71, for a gas solenoid valve drive circuit abnormality caused by a circuit board failure.

Likely causes

  • A loose connector on one of the gas solenoid valves (SV0 on the gas valve, or SV1 to SV3 on the manifold plate)
  • Damaged gas solenoid valve wiring or connections
  • A failed gas solenoid valve coil
  • A failed circuit board (the gas solenoid valve drive circuit)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance and shut off its gas valve before working on it; never disconnect or alter the gas line. If you smell gas, leave and call your gas utility.

  1. Turn the heater's power off and back on. Noritz's error table says that is how code 51 is reset.
  2. If 51 comes right back, stop resetting it and call for service. The fix involves the gas valve, manifold plate or circuit board inside the unit.
  3. Don't open the gas valve or manifold yourself. Noritz lists 51 in its technician service documents, not in the homeowner troubleshooting steps.
  4. Write down your model number from the rating label and whether the display shows 51 or 511, so the technician can bring the right parts.
  5. If you ever smell gas, leave the area and call your gas utility or a qualified technician right away.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • Checking whether any of the SV0 to SV3 connectors on the manifold plate and gas valve have come loose, and reconnecting them.
  • Measuring the coil resistance of each gas solenoid valve with the connector unplugged, per the Noritz diagnostic flow chart.
  • Replacing the gas valve if SV0 is faulty, or the manifold plate if SV1, SV2 or SV3 is faulty.
  • Replacing the circuit board if the valves and wiring test normal (Noritz says the display window board does not need replacing).
