Code: E63🌀 Bosch Dryer

Bosch Dryer E63 Error Code: Heating Element Failure or Power Unit Fault

Reviewed 2026-10-25 · Based on 3 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

E63 on a Bosch dryer is a fault that Bosch describes in two ways depending on the region and model. Bosch's US guide says it indicates a heating element failure, while Bosch France and Bosch's service error list for at least one US model (WTG865H2UC) tie it to the power unit, the power electronics module that also drives the motor. Unplug the dryer, try one full power reset (power off for at least 5 minutes), and if E63 comes back, book Bosch service with your model number (E-Nr).

What the code means

Bosch's US dryer error code guide says E63 indicates a heating element failure. Its advice is to unplug the dryer, inspect the heating element for visible damage or burns, make sure the heating element connections are secure, and contact an authorized technician if nothing obvious turns up. Bosch France's dedicated E63 page describes it differently: the motor may be disconnected from the power unit, or the power module may be defective, and it recommends calling Bosch's technicians. The technician reference MyPros+, quoting the Bosch WTG865H2UC service manual error list, matches the French wording: E:63 is the power unit's collective number for system errors, and on models with Basic Software 240 it may appear instead of E:90, with the first check being the electrical connections between the motor and the power unit. In plain terms, E63 means the dryer's power electronics have flagged a problem with a load they drive (the heater or the motor), so the dryer stops. Which one is more likely depends on your model, which is why the technician should confirm it from the E-Nr. E63 is different from E06, which points to the heating circuit overheating, and from E90, the general electronic fault (see our Bosch E06 and E90 pages).

Likely causes

  • A failed heating element or loose heating element connections, which is how Bosch's US error code guide describes E63
  • The motor disconnected from the power unit, or a defective power module, which is the wording on Bosch France's E63 page
  • A power unit system error, which the Bosch WTG865H2UC service error list (via MyPros+) says E:63 collects and may show instead of E:90 on some software versions
  • A temporary electronics glitch after a power interruption, which a 5-minute power reset can sometimes clear (Bosch US guide)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before opening panels.

  1. Turn the dryer off and note the exact code and your model number (E-Nr) from the rating label inside the door.
  2. Power reset: unplug the dryer or switch off its circuit breaker for at least 5 minutes, then restore power. Bosch says a power cycle can often clear error codes and reset the electronics.
  3. Run a short program with a small load. If it finishes normally, watch the next few loads.
  4. If E63 returns, keep the dryer unplugged. Bosch's US guide suggests checking the heating element for visible damage or burns and making sure its connections are secure, but the heater, motor and power unit sit inside the cabinet. If you aren't comfortable working on an unplugged appliance's internal wiring, leave this step to a technician.
  5. Don't run the dryer repeatedly with E63 showing, and never bypass any thermal protection or reconnect wiring you're unsure about.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • E63 comes back after a 5-minute power reset: call Bosch Customer Support at 1-800-944-2904 (US) or book an authorized Bosch technician, and give them the E-Nr.
  • Tell the technician that Bosch's sources link E63 both to the heating element and to the power unit and motor connection, so they can check the heater, the motor-to-power-unit wiring and the power module for your specific model.
  • The dryer smells of burning, trips the breaker or shows visible scorch marks: unplug it and leave it off until it's been inspected.
