Code: E01🧺 Smeg Dishwasher

Smeg Dishwasher E01 (E1) Error Code: Anti-Flood (Aquastop) System Tripped

Reviewed 2026-10-26 · Based on 4 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

E01 (shown as E 01 or E1 on some models) on a Smeg dishwasher means the anti-flooding system, called Aquastop, has tripped. Water has got into the tray in the base and lifted the float switch, so the dishwasher stops the program and runs the drain pump. Smeg's manuals say E1 needs the technical assistance service rather than a simple restart. You can safely switch off, turn the tap off and look for the cause of the water, but the leak itself usually needs an engineer.

What the code means

Smeg dishwasher user manuals (for example DF344BW and DF344AX) list E 01 in the self-diagnostics table as "The anti-flooding system (where present) has been tripped", and say that alarms E1 and E11 require you to contact the technical assistance service; for most other alarms you switch off, switch back on and restart. Older Smeg models that show faults with indicator lights (for example DI6013-1) call E1 "Acquastop failure" and say the system trips in case of water leaks. Smeg's service fault-code notes (Smeg Service UK, PCB fault codes) give the mechanism: E1 is generated when the float switch of the anti-overflow system is held closed by water in the base tray. While it is closed the drain pump keeps running until the switch opens again. The engineer's job is to find where the water came from: the tub, the Aquastop hose, or the parts in the base such as the wash pump, drain pump or water softener. If there is no water in the tray, the float may simply be sitting in the wrong position and holding the switch closed. Note that E01 here is a different code from Smeg's E 02 (water level limit), E 05 (no water intake) and E 06 (not draining).

Likely causes

  • Water leaking into the base tray, which lifts the anti-flood float switch
  • Leak from the tub, door seal area or a cracked sump
  • Leak from the Aquastop inlet hose or its connection
  • Leak from a component in the base: wash pump, drain pump or water softener
  • Float stuck or out of position holding the switch closed when the tray is actually dry

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Switch the dishwasher off at the wall and turn off the water tap feeding it.
  2. Check the floor and the area around the door and the inlet hose connection for drips, and tighten the hose at the tap if it's loose.
  3. Leave the machine off for a day so any water in the base tray can dry out.
  4. Turn the tap back on, switch on and run a short program while you watch for leaks. If E01 returns, stop and call Smeg service, as the manuals say.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • E01 comes back after the base has dried out: Smeg says E1 needs the technical assistance service.
  • You can see water coming from under the machine or from the Aquastop hose.
  • There's no visible leak at all but E01 persists: the float position or switch needs checking.
  • The machine is under warranty, or it's built in and you can't safely move it.
