Maytag Washer Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Maytag washer code explained on FixCodes, 6 in all. Together they cite 32 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
5dToo Many Suds
5d on a Maytag washer is really Sd, short for excess suds (front loaders may show Sud).
CLTime to Run the Clean Washer Cycle
CL on a Maytag washer isn't a fault.
F5 E3Door or Lid Lock Error
F5 E3 on a Maytag washer is a door or lid lock error.
F7 E1Motor or Basket Speed Error
F7E1 on a Maytag washer means the control can't sense or reach the right drum or basket speed.
F9 E1Long Drain Time
F9 E1 on a Maytag washer means a long drain: the washer is taking too long to pump the water out, typically more than eight minutes.
LFLong Fill, Taking Too Long to Fill
LF on a Maytag washer means long fill: the washer is taking too long to fill, the same problem other Maytag models show as F8 E1 (or Lo FL on front loaders).

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
