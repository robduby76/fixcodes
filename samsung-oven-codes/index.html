Samsung Oven Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Samsung oven code explained on FixCodes, 8 in all. Together they cite 23 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
C-21Oven Got Too Hot (Overheat Safety Error)
C-21 on a Samsung range or wall oven means the oven's temperature sensor saw the cavity get hotter than it should: Samsung's US owner's manuals say it appears when the…
C-24Oven Heating Up Too Fast, Usually a Blocked Vent
C-24 on a Samsung range or wall oven means the temperature sensor saw the oven heating up faster than normal.
C-A2Cooling Motor Operating Abnormally
C-A2 on a Samsung wall oven means the cooling motor (the fan that keeps the controls and electronics cool) is operating abnormally.
C-d0Stuck or Shorted Button on the Control Panel
C-d0 on a Samsung oven or range means the control thinks a button has been pressed and held too long.
C-F0Main and Sub Board Communication Error
C-F0 on a Samsung range or wall oven means the oven's main control board and sub control board have stopped talking to each other.
C-F2Touch Panel Communication Error
C-F2 on a Samsung range or wall oven means the touch controls stopped talking to the control board.
E-08Oven Heating Error (Not Heating Up)
E-08 on a Samsung oven is an oven heating error: the control didn't see the temperature rise the way it expected.
