Gree Mini Split Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Gree mini split code explained on FixCodes, 13 in all. Together they cite 15 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
E4High Discharge Temperature Protection
E4 on a Gree mini split means the compressor's discharge line got too hot, so the outdoor unit shut the compressor down to protect it.
E6Indoor and Outdoor Unit Communication Error
E6 on a Gree mini split means the indoor and outdoor units have stopped talking to each other.
E7Mode Conflict Between Indoor Units
E7 on a Gree multi-zone mini split means mode conflict: two indoor heads are asking the one outdoor unit for modes it can't run at the same time, such as one room on…
EARefrigerant Leak Detected
EA on a Gree mini split means the refrigerant sensor in the indoor unit has detected an R-32 refrigerant leak.
F0Refrigerant Protection, Low Refrigerant or Leak
F0 on a Gree mini split means the system has shut itself down in refrigerant protection mode because it thinks the refrigerant charge is too low.

H4 on a Gree mini split is an overload or high-temperature protection: the outdoor unit sensed its coil (tube) temperature running above the protection limit and shut…
H5IPM Module Protection
H5 on a Gree mini split means IPM protection, also called module protection: the outdoor board's inverter power module (the part that drives the compressor) has tripped…
H6Indoor Fan Motor Not Running or No Feedback
H6 on a Gree mini split means the indoor unit's fan motor isn't running or the control board isn't getting a speed signal back from it.
H7Compressor Desynchronizing (Out of Step) Protection
H7 on a Gree mini split means the outdoor board lost track of the inverter compressor's rotor position, so the compressor fell out of step with its drive signal.
HCPFC Protection
HC on a Gree mini split means PFC protection: the power factor correction circuit on the outdoor inverter board has tripped.

L3 on a Gree mini split means the outdoor unit's fan, a DC fan motor, isn't running the way the control board expects.
