Code: 5d🧺 Maytag Washer

Maytag Washer 5d Error Code (Sd, Sud): Too Many Suds (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-10-23 · Based on 6 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

5d on a Maytag washer is really Sd, short for excess suds (front loaders may show Sud). Maytag says the washer is running a suds reduction routine, so the first step is to let it continue. The usual cause is non-HE detergent, too much detergent, a big pac on a small load, or thickened "no splash" bleach; on front loaders a kinked or clogged drain hose can also trigger it.

What the code means

Maytag's top-load help page says Sd may appear as 5d, and that Sd or Sud means there are excessive suds in the washer and it is running a suds reduction routine. On front loaders, Maytag explains that the washer can't spin water out through heavy suds, so it extends the rinse: it pauses tumbling, adds water and lets the bubbles break, and that pause and rinse may happen several times. Maytag's front-load suds guide adds that Sud or Sd with the Rinse light on means the washer is still removing suds, while Sud or Sd with Cycle Complete lit means the load may not be fully rinsed or spun out. The Whirlpool/Maytag top-load technical sheet W10092760 describes how top loaders sense it: the control measures drag on the basket, shows Sd steady while it runs a mini clean-out cycle, and flashes Sd and ends the cycle if the clean-out can't fix the over-sudsing. That sheet also lists a bunched or twisted load and a basket that can't engage during drain (mechanical friction in the drive) as possible reasons, so it isn't always the detergent.

Likely causes

  • Non-HE detergent, or more HE detergent than its label calls for (Maytag notes HE detergents range from 2x to 8x concentration)
  • A large pre-measured detergent pac on a small or lightly soiled load
  • "No splash" or "easy pour" liquid chlorine bleach, which contains a thickener that can make extra suds
  • Bulk dispenser enabled (or set to the wrong concentration) while detergent is also added by hand
  • Detergent buildup inside the washer
  • Front loaders: a kinked or clogged drain hose or a restricted standpipe
  • Top loaders: a load bunched or twisted in the basket, or drive friction that stops the basket engaging during drain (per tech sheet W10092760)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Let the cycle continue. The washer is running its own suds reduction routine, and the pauses and extra rinses are normal.
  2. If the cycle finishes with Cycle Complete lit and the clothes still soapy or wet, run a Rinse/Spin cycle with no detergent.
  3. Use only HE detergent and measure it by the detergent maker's dose for your load size and soil level. A more concentrated detergent needs less.
  4. Save large detergent pacs for large or heavily soiled loads.
  5. If you used "no splash" or "easy pour" bleach, switch to regular liquid chlorine bleach. Maytag suggests running the washer empty with regular bleach or affresh washer cleaner before the next load.
  6. If your washer has a bulk dispenser, don't add detergent by hand while it's enabled, and check that the concentration setting matches your detergent.
  7. Run a Clean Washer cycle after the load is done to remove detergent buildup. Maytag recommends cleaning the washer about once a month, or more often with hard water or heavy use.
  8. Front loaders: make sure the drain hose isn't kinked or clogged and is installed in the standpipe correctly.
  9. Top loaders: if the code shows mid-wash, open the lid and spread out a load that has bunched or twisted.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • Sd or Sud keeps coming back even with the right dose of HE detergent, regular bleach and a freshly cleaned washer.
  • On a top loader, the basket doesn't seem to engage or spin during drain. The tech sheet points to friction in the drive, which needs a technician.
  • The washer won't drain even after the suds are gone, or it moves on to a drain code such as F9 E1.
  • The washer is under warranty. Use Maytag's contact or schedule-service page rather than opening it up.
