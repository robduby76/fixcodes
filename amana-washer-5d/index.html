Code: 5d🧺 Amana Washer

Amana Washer 5d Error Code (Sd, Sud): Too Many Suds (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-10-25 · Based on 4 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

5d on an Amana washer is really Sd, short for excess suds (some models show Sud). Amana says the washer is running a suds reduction routine, so the first step is to let it keep going. The usual cause is non-HE detergent, too much detergent, a big pac on a small load, detergent buildup, or thickened "no splash" bleach; on front loaders a kinked or clogged drain hose can also trigger it.

What the code means

Amana's top-load help article says Sd may appear as 5d, and that Sd or Sud (excess suds) means there are excessive suds in the washer and it is running a suds reduction routine, so you should allow it to continue. Amana's front-load error code list explains why: the washer can't spin water out through heavy suds, so it automatically extends the rinse, pausing the tumbling and adding water so the bubbles can break, and that pause and rinse may happen several times. Amana's SUD/SD article adds that the cycle may run longer while it clears the suds, and that you may see flashing lights, an alert tone, or foam left in the drum after the cycle. It is a usage warning, not a parts failure, in most cases.

Likely causes

  • Non-HE detergent, which Amana names as a common cause
  • Too much HE detergent for the load; Amana notes HE detergents range from 2x to 8x concentration, so the right dose varies
  • A large pre-measured detergent pac on a small or lightly soiled load
  • Detergent buildup inside the washer
  • "No splash" or "easy pour" liquid chlorine bleach, which contains a thickener that can make extra suds
  • Front loaders: a kinked or clogged drain hose or a restricted standpipe (drainpipe)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Let the cycle continue. The washer is running its own suds reduction routine, and the pauses and extra rinses are normal.
  2. If the load still feels soapy when the cycle ends, run a rinse and spin with no detergent.
  3. Use HE detergent and measure it by the detergent maker's dose for your load size and soil level. A more concentrated detergent needs less.
  4. Save large detergent pacs for large or heavily soiled loads.
  5. If you used "no splash" or "easy pour" bleach, Amana says to run the washer empty with regular liquid bleach or affresh washer cleaner before the next load.
  6. Clean the washer to clear detergent buildup. Amana recommends cleaning at least once a month, more often with hard water or heavy use.
  7. Don't overload the washer, and make sure it is level so it drains properly, as Amana's SUD article advises.
  8. Front loaders: check that the drain hose isn't kinked or clogged and is installed in the standpipe correctly.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • Sd or Sud keeps coming back even with the right dose of HE detergent, regular bleach, and a freshly cleaned washer. Amana says to contact customer support or a qualified technician at that point.
  • The washer won't drain even after the suds are gone, or it moves on to a drain code such as F9 E1.
  • The washer is under warranty. Use Amana's contact or schedule-service page (1-866-616-2664 in the US) rather than opening it up.
