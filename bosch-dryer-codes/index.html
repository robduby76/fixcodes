Bosch Dryer Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Bosch dryer code explained on FixCodes, 14 in all. Together they cite 32 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
CrERun the Appliance Care Programme (Not a Fault)
CrE on a Bosch dryer is not an error.
DRDoor Not Closed or Condensation Water Not Draining
DR on a Bosch dryer has two meanings depending on the model.
E01Blocked Lint Filter or Heat Exchanger
E01 on a Bosch dryer means airflow is blocked at the lint (fluff) filter.
E03Condensate Drain Hose Blocked or Kinked
E03 on a Bosch condensation or heat pump dryer means the water the dryer pulls out of your clothes can't drain away: Bosch says the condensation drainage hose is blocked…
E06Heating Circuit / Heating Element Overheating
E06 on a Bosch dryer points to a fault in the heating circuit.
E08Drum Temperature Sensor (NTC) / Electronic Fault
E08 on a Bosch dryer is an electronic fault that Bosch says only an authorized technician can fix.
E09Temperature Sensor (NTC) / Electronic Fault
E09 on a Bosch dryer is an electronic fault that Bosch says only an authorized technician can fix.
E12Overheating From Restricted Airflow
E12 on a Bosch dryer means the dryer is overheating because air can't move through it freely.
E20Control Module Memory or Software Fault
E20 on a Bosch dryer is a control module fault: Bosch service material for WTMC dryers describes it as an EEPROM (memory) error or an invalid software update, and the…
E24Electronic Fault (Heat Pump Inverter or Software Mismatch)
E24 on a Bosch dryer is an electronics fault that Bosch says only an authorized technician can fix.
E28Temperature Sensor (NTC) Circuit Fault
E28 on a Bosch dryer is an electronic fault that, in Bosch service material, points to a temperature sensor (NTC) circuit: the sensor or its wiring is open or shorted.
E56Control Panel Communication Fault
E56 on a Bosch dryer most often means the main electronics (power module) can't talk to the control panel electronics (operating module).
E63Heating Element Failure or Power Unit Fault
E63 on a Bosch dryer is a fault that Bosch describes in two ways depending on the region and model.
E90Electronic Fault
E90 on a Bosch dryer means the dryer's electronics have detected a fault.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
