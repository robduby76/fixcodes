Candy Washing Machine E01 Error Code: Door Lock Fault

Reviewed 2026-10-27 · Based on 3 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

E01 (shown as E:01 on some displays) on a Candy washing machine means the machine can't confirm the door is closed and locked, so it won't start or carry on the wash. First open the door, check nothing is caught in the door seal, and shut it firmly until it clicks. Then unplug the machine for a couple of minutes and try again. If E01 keeps coming back with the door properly shut, the door lock (interlock) or its wiring has usually failed.

What the code means

Candy Hoover Service's technical note 00188/LB, which covers Candy Smart Touch and Hoover One Touch washing machines with the Invensys power board, lists E:01 as "Door Safety Lock Device/Drum's Braking device defective and/or Wiring". The door safety lock is the interlock that holds the door shut during a wash and tells the board it's safe to fill and spin. If the board never gets that confirmation, it stops the programme and shows E01. On Candy models with only LEDs, the same note says four LEDs flash together once a second for the number of the error (once for E01), then a long pause, repeating until the machine is switched off. Whitegoods Help describes E01 the same way, as the machine not detecting that the door is closed and locked, and names a worn interlock as the usual cause after five to ten years of use, followed by wiring faults, a trapped door seal and a broken handle or catch. Note that Candy's newer user manuals (for example the UM02 manual) use E7 for door problems instead, with the same advice to shut the door properly and unplug for 2-3 minutes if it's stuck locked. The same technical note lists E:07 as a door lock stuck closed, so check which code your model actually shows.

Likely causes

  • Door not fully shut, often because clothing or the door seal is caught in the opening
  • Worn or failed door safety lock (interlock), the most common cause on older machines
  • Loose or damaged wiring between the door lock and the main board
  • Broken door handle, hinge or catch (door hook) so the door can't latch
  • Less often, a fault on the main board's door-lock circuit

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Open the door, push any clothes back into the drum, make sure the rubber seal isn't folded over, and close the door firmly until you hear it click.
  2. Switch off and unplug the machine for 2-3 minutes, then plug in and start a short programme. Candy's manual gives the same unplug-and-wait step if the door is stuck locked.
  3. Look at the door catch (the hook on the door) for cracks or a bent tip and check it lines up with the slot in the front panel. A sagging door from a worn hinge can stop it latching.
  4. If the door won't open after an error and there's water in the drum, drain it first through the pump filter at the bottom front, with towels and a tray down.
  5. If you're comfortable working on an unplugged machine, Whitegoods Help suggests testing the door interlock for continuity; check its plug is fully seated and the wires to it aren't chafed or burnt, and compare readings against the wiring diagram for your model.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • E01 keeps coming back with the door properly closed and the catch in good shape, and you're not comfortable replacing the door lock yourself.
  • A new door lock doesn't clear it, which points to wiring or the main board.
  • The machine is under warranty: contact Candy's customer service rather than opening it.
