Whirlpool Washer Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Whirlpool washer code explained on FixCodes, 7 in all. Together they cite 32 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
5dToo Many Suds
5d on a Whirlpool washer is really Sd, short for suds detected (front loaders may show Sud).
F3 E1Water Level (Pressure Sensor) Can't Be Read
F3 E1 (also shown as F3E1) on a Whirlpool washer means the control can't detect the water level: the pressure signal it uses to judge how full the tub is is missing or…
F5 E3Door Lock Error, Door Won't Lock or Unlock
F5 E3 on a Whirlpool front-load washer is a door lock error.
F7 E1Motor Speed Sensing Error, Washer Can't Reach Spin Speed
F7 E1 on a Whirlpool washer means the control can't sense or reach the right motor or basket speed.
F9 E1Long Drain Time
F9 E1 on a Whirlpool front-load washer means a long drain: the washer took too long to pump out the water, typically more than 8 minutes, so it stopped.
LFLong Fill, Taking Too Long to Fill
LF (long fill) on a Whirlpool washer means the washer is taking too long to fill.
LOC / LCControl Lock Is On (How to Unlock)
LOC (or LC on some models) on a Whirlpool washer isn't a fault.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
