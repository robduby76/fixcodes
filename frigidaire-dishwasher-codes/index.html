Frigidaire Dishwasher Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Frigidaire dishwasher code explained on FixCodes, 3 in all. Together they cite 16 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
i20Not Draining Completely
i20 on a Frigidaire dishwasher means the unit didn't drain completely.
i30Leak or Overflow in the Bottom Pan
i30 on a Frigidaire dishwasher means water has been detected in the bottom (base) pan under the tub, from a leak or an overflow.
PFPower Was Interrupted
PF on a Frigidaire dishwasher means the power was interrupted, or the dishwasher was just installed.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
