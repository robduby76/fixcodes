Kenmore Washer Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Kenmore washer code explained on FixCodes, 8 in all. Together they cite 23 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
dE1Door Open or Not Locking (dE, dE1, dE2)
dE1 on a Kenmore front-load washer means the control thinks the door is open, so the washer won't start or keep running.
F5Lid Lock or Lid Switch Fault (F5 E1, F5 E2, F5 E3, F5 E4)
F5 on a Kenmore top-load washer points to the lid lock and lid switch.
F21Drain Problem, Long Drain
F21 on a Kenmore front-load washer means it couldn't drain in time.
IEWater Inlet Error, Not Filling
IE on a Kenmore washer is a water inlet error: the washer didn't fill to the expected level in time.
LELocked Motor, Drum Won't Turn
LE on a Kenmore washer is a locked motor error: the control tried to turn the drum but didn't sense it moving.
LFLong Fill, Taking Too Long to Fill
LF on a Kenmore washer means long fill: the washer didn't reach its expected water level in the time it allows.
OEDrain Error, Washer Won't Drain
OE on a Kenmore washer is a drain error: the washer didn't finish draining in time.
UEUnbalanced Load, Won't Spin
UE on a Kenmore washer means the load is out of balance and the washer gave up trying to spin it.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
