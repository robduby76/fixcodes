Code: E21🧺 Bosch Washer

Bosch Washer E21 (F21, F:21) Error Code: Motor Fault, Drum Not Turning (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-11-07 · Based on 5 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

On Bosch washing machines, E21 / F21 (printed F:21 in Bosch manuals) is a motor fault: the machine tried to turn the drum and the motor didn't run the way the control expected. Bosch's own instruction for this code is short: call the after-sales service. You can switch the machine off and on once, unplug it, check the drum turns freely by hand and look for an overload, but a code that comes back needs a motor check, and on older brushed motors the usual repair is worn carbon brushes.

What the code means

Bosch prints this code as F:21 in the display tables of its older WAE-series manuals, and many machines show the same code as E21 or F21. In the instruction manual for the WAE24377GB (Bosch document 9001021008), the "Information on the display panel" table says: F:21 "Motor fault. Call the after-sales service." The Australian manual for the WAE22460AU, WAE24460AU and WAE26470AU (document 9000221576, page 8) gives the same meaning: F:21 "Motor fault. Call customer service!" Neither manual lists anything the owner should fix for F:21, unlike F:17 (water supply) and F:18 (drain), which have owner checks. In plain terms, the control board switched the drive motor on but didn't see the drum turning at the right speed, so it stopped the programme. Bosch UK's "drum is not turning" help page says that if the drum doesn't turn when filled with water or during spin, this can indicate worn carbon brushes, which need repair by a trained Bosch engineer, and Bosch Australia says a drum that won't turn has causes you can't fix yourself and to call service. Bosch US doesn't list 21 on its washer error-code page; its general advice for any other fault code is to switch the machine off and on, then disconnect it from power for at least 30 seconds, and call service if the message comes back. Newer Bosch machines with E:xx / -xx codes report drive problems differently (Bosch US lists E80 as a fault in the drive circuit), so if your display shows a two-part code, check your own manual. If the drum turns but the machine won't drain, see our Bosch E18 guide instead; if it won't fill, see Bosch E17.

Likely causes

  • Motor fault: the drive motor didn't run or didn't report the right speed to the control (Bosch manuals 9001021008 and 9000221576: F:21 motor fault)
  • Worn motor carbon brushes on brushed motors, so the motor can't get power (Bosch UK: drum not turning when full of water or on spin can indicate worn carbon brushes)
  • A loose or damaged connection between the motor and the control board, or a fault in the motor winding or its speed sensor (tacho); these need a technician's meter checks
  • A fault in the motor control on the electronic board, usually found only after the motor and wiring test good
  • Something stopping the drum from turning freely, such as an item jammed between the drum and the tub, an overloaded drum or a very heavy single item (general causes; Bosch doesn't list these for F:21)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Turn the programme selector to Off and unplug the machine. Bosch's general fault steps are to switch off and back on first; Bosch US says if a code returns, disconnect the power for at least 30 seconds and reconnect. If the code was a one-off it will disappear.
  2. If there's water in the drum and the door won't open, don't force it. Let the water cool, then drain it through the drain pump filter as your manual describes before you open the door.
  3. With the machine unplugged, open the door and turn the drum by hand. It should turn smoothly. If it grinds, sticks or won't move, stop there: something may be jammed between the drum and the tub, or a bearing may have failed, and both need a technician.
  4. Take out part of the load if the drum is packed or holds one heavy item like a soaked duvet, spread the rest out, and try a short programme once.
  5. If F21 / E21 comes back, stop running cycles. Bosch's manuals say to call the after-sales service for this code, and repeated attempts with a failing motor can make the damage worse.
  6. Write down the E-Nr (product number) and FD (production number) before you call. Bosch's WAE24377GB manual says they're on the inside of the door or the service flap and on the back of the machine. Bosch Customer Support in the US is 1-800-944-2904 and Bosch Australia is 1300 369 744.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • Every F21 / E21 that returns after a power reset. Both Bosch manuals say to call the after-sales service for a motor fault.
  • The drum won't turn by hand, grinds, or you smell burning or see sparking from under the machine.
  • Checking the carbon brushes, motor windings, motor wiring and the board's motor control. Bosch UK says worn carbon brushes need a trained Bosch engineer, and these checks are done with the machine unplugged and partly dismantled.
  • The machine is under warranty. Let Bosch service inspect it before anyone else opens it.
