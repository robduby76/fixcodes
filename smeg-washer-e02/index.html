Smeg Washing Machine E02 Error Code: Low Water Pressure or Water Level

Reviewed 2026-11-05 · Based on 4 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

On Smeg washing machines that use the E01 to E04 code table (such as the LBW50CIT), E02 means the water pressure or the water level inside the machine is low, so the wash can't fill properly. Smeg's manual says to check the tap is fully open and that the mains water hasn't been cut off; if it continues, the machine stops on its own, and you should unplug it, turn the tap off and contact an authorised service agent. Cleaning the water inlet filters (which Smeg recommends when the machine can't get enough water with the tap open) often clears it.

What the code means

Smeg's LBW50CIT user manual, section 10 "Automatic fault warnings and what to do", shows E02 as a combination of flashing wash-stage lights and gives the possible fault as "the water pressure or the water level inside the machine may be low". The instruction is to check the tap is fully turned on (mains water may be cut off); if the problem continues, the machine will stop after a while automatically, and you should unplug it, turn off the tap and contact the nearest authorised service agent. In the same table E01 is the door not shut properly, E03 is a pump or pump filter problem, and E04 is too much water in the machine. The manual's general troubleshooting table for "your machine does not take in water" lists the same practical causes: tap off, inlet hose twisted or clogged, inlet filter clogged, or door not fully closed. Section 7.2 says the water inlet filters stop dirt entering the machine and should be cleaned when the machine can't get enough water even though the supply is on and the tap is open (Smeg recommends every 2 months). Installation section 3.4 specifies water flow with pressure of 0.1–1 MPa. Smeg UK's washing machine and washer dryer help page puts E02 in the group of codes that need an engineer visit if the simple checks don't clear it. Not every Smeg uses this table: on the code list UK Whitegoods publishes for many Smeg machines from about 2003 on, E2 is listed as an anti-spilling device fault (check water supply, air chamber/pressure sensor hose, inlet valve and wiring, Aquastop hose where fitted, and the main control PCB), so check the error table in your own manual (model number is on the label inside the door).

Likely causes

  • Water tap closed or only partly open
  • Mains water supply cut off, frozen or with very low pressure (Smeg's install range is about 0.1–1 MPa)
  • Inlet hose kinked, twisted, crushed or blocked
  • Clogged water inlet filters at the valve and/or in the tap end of the hose (Smeg's first maintenance step when fill is weak with the tap open)
  • Door not fully closed, so the machine won't fill
  • Faulty water inlet valve, pressure switch / air chamber hose, Aquastop hose (where fitted), wiring or main control board (engineer-level)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Make sure the cold water tap (and hot, if your machine has a hot inlet) feeding the washer is fully open, and that other taps in the house have normal pressure.
  2. Pull the machine out and check the inlet hose along its whole length for kinks, crushing or freezing. Straighten or replace a damaged hose; Smeg says to use only the new hose supplied with the machine and not to reuse old ones.
  3. If the door isn't shut firmly until it clicks, close it properly — the machine won't fill with the door open (the same check as for E01 / OPEN).
  4. Turn the machine off, unplug it and turn the water tap off. Unscrew the inlet hose from the back of the machine. Using long-nosed pliers, gently pull the plastic bar to remove the mesh filter from the water inlet valve, and remove the second filter from the tap end of the hose. Clean both thoroughly with a soft brush, rinse, and refit. Smeg recommends this when the machine can't get enough water with the tap open.
  5. Reconnect the hose, open the tap, check for leaks, plug the machine back in and run a short wash. If E02 returns with a clear supply and clean filters, stop there: Smeg's instruction is to unplug, turn the tap off and contact an authorised service agent.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • E02 comes back after the tap, hose and inlet-filter checks, which Smeg says needs an authorised service agent.
  • There is no water at the tap, or household pressure is clearly too low for other fixtures as well — that may need a plumber before the washer can fill.
  • You hear no click from the inlet valve when a fill should start, or the machine fills for a while then still shows E02, which points to the valve, pressure sensing or the board.
  • The machine is under warranty: Smeg Service UK lists 0330 094 7779 for in-warranty repairs, and Smeg also has a find-an-engineer service for out-of-warranty machines.
