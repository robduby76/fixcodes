Code: E29🧺 Bosch Washer

Bosch Washer E29 (F29, E00-29) Error Code: Not Enough Water Coming In (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-10-24 · Based on 4 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

E29 (also shown as F29 or E00-29) on a Bosch washer means too little water is reaching the machine. Bosch points to two causes: water pressure that is too low, or blocked filters in the water connection or AquaStop hose. Open the tap fully, check that about 10 liters (2.5 gallons) per minute comes out, and clean the inlet filters with a small brush. Never put an AquaStop hose in water, because it contains an electric valve. If E29 keeps coming back with good flow, book a Bosch technician.

What the code means

Bosch France titles its E29 / F29 help page "check the water tap and clean the inlet filters," and lists two causes: the water pressure is too low, or the filters in the water connection of the inlet hose or AquaStop are clogged. Bosch US groups F29 with E17 and F17 under the same message: the filters in the water connection or AquaStop hose are blocked or the water pressure is too low. Bosch Singapore uses the same grouping. On washers with a three-digit display Bosch lists the code as E29 / F29 / E00-29. Because Bosch treats F29 the same way as E17, the steps below match our Bosch E17 page. Code lists differ between model families, so check your own owner's manual too.

Likely causes

  • The water tap is turned off or only partly open
  • Water pressure that is too low (Bosch says about 10 liters, or 2.5 gallons, per minute should flow from the fully open tap)
  • A clogged filter where the inlet hose connects to the tap
  • On models without AquaStop, a clogged filter where the hose connects to the back of the washer
  • A kinked or pinched inlet hose behind the machine
  • A fault inside the washer, such as the inlet valve, if E29 stays with good water flow and clean filters, which needs a Bosch technician to diagnose

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Turn the washer off and unplug it.
  2. Make sure the water tap is fully open and the inlet hose behind the washer isn't kinked or squashed.
  3. Turn the tap off and unscrew the inlet hose from the tap.
  4. Clean the filter in the hose connection with a small brush under running water. If you have an AquaStop hose, don't put it in water: Bosch warns it contains an electric valve.
  5. On models without AquaStop, also remove the hose from the back of the washer, pull out the filter there with pliers and clean it.
  6. Check the pressure: hold a bucket under the tap and open it fully. About 10 liters (2.5 gallons) should come out in one minute. If it's much less, call a plumber, since Bosch says low pressure is a plumbing issue.
  7. Reconnect the hose, open the tap fully, check for leaks, plug in and restart the program.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • E29 keeps coming back even though the tap is fully open, the filters are clean and you measured about 10 liters per minute. Bosch France says to schedule a Bosch technician.
  • Your home's water flow is well under 10 liters per minute at the tap, which Bosch says needs a plumber.
  • The AquaStop hose is damaged or leaking at the tap end.
  • The washer is under warranty. Bosch Customer Support in the US is at 1-800-944-2904.
