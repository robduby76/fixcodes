Electrolux Washer Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Electrolux washer code explained on FixCodes, 4 in all. Together they cite 15 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
E11Not Filling or Fill Time Too Long
E11 on an Electrolux washer means the machine isn't filling with water, or is filling too slowly, during the wash phase.
E21Water Not Draining Fast Enough
E21 on an Electrolux washer means a drain problem: the water isn't pumping out fast enough or is taking too long to empty.
E35Water Overfill (Water Level Too High)
E35 on an Electrolux front-load washer means the pressure sensor is reading too much water in the tub, an overfill.
E53Motor Control Voltage or Motor Triac Sensing Fault
E53 on an Electrolux front-load washer is a motor drive electronics fault, not a clogged filter or an unbalanced load.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
