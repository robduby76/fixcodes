Code: 20🚿 Noritz Tankless Water Heater

Noritz Error Code 20: High Limit Switch Tripped (Overheat)

Reviewed 2026-10-24 · Based on 3 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

Error code 20 (EC20) on a Noritz tankless water heater means the high limit switch has tripped because the water in or leaving the heat exchanger got dangerously hot. Noritz says the most common cause is scale build-up inside the heat exchanger, so a descale flush is usually the first fix. Other causes are poor combustion, a blocked vent, a loose wire at the switch, or a failed high limit switch. The code usually needs a hard reset (unplugging or cutting power).

What the code means

The high limit switch is a safety switch mounted on an elbow of the copper heat exchanger, or on the copper pipe leaving the heat exchanger on pre-mix style units. Noritz says it trips when the water approaches roughly 176°F to 205°F, using switches rated 80°C, 94°C or 96°C depending on the unit (smaller units trip lower, larger and commercial units higher). When it trips, combustion stops immediately and the unit closes its water control servo so superheated water can't leave the heater. Scale reduces how well heat passes into the water, so the heat exchanger and other parts hold the heat and overheat. Noritz warns that repeatedly resetting code 20 without fixing the cause can lead to error code 14, which is not resettable and needs a much bigger repair. If the switch or its wiring has failed, the code usually won't clear even when the unit is cold. Noritz's Pro Series models use code 20 for the same high limit switch trip.

Likely causes

  • Scale (hard-water mineral) build-up in the heat exchanger, the most common cause
  • Dust, dirt or lint build-up causing poor combustion
  • An obstruction in the venting that keeps hot exhaust from leaving
  • A loose or bad wiring connection between the high limit switch and the main wire harness
  • A failed high limit switch

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Turn the heater off, unplug it or switch off its power, and let it cool for a while. Noritz says that if the switch and wiring are good, the code usually resets when power comes back on and the unit will try to run normally.
  2. If the code won't clear even with the unit cold, suspect the switch wiring or the switch itself and call a technician rather than resetting over and over.
  3. If you have hard water with no softener or treatment and no record of descale flushes, flush the heat exchanger. Noritz's owner's guide says periodic flushing is necessary in hard-water areas, that scale can damage the heat exchanger, and that scale damage isn't covered by the warranty. Follow the flushing procedure in your model's manual; newer models have a Scale Flushing menu item.
  4. Check the outdoor vent and air intake terminations for blockages such as nests, leaves, snow or ice.
  5. Look for dust, dirt or lint around the unit. Noritz recommends a combustion cleaning if there are signs of build-up, which is a job for a technician.
  6. Keep the temperature setting as low as practical; Noritz notes that lower settings help prevent scale build-up.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • Have a technician check the high limit switch and its connection to the main wire harness if the code won't reset cold, and replace the switch if it has failed.
  • Call for a full service (descale flush plus combustion cleaning) if code 20 keeps coming back, before it turns into a non-resettable code 14.
  • Noritz technical assistance is at 866-766-7489, and its Contractor Locator can find a local service representative.
