Code: E15🧺 Hotpoint Washer

Hotpoint Washer Dryer E15 Error Code: Water Will Not Pump Out

Reviewed 2026-10-26 · Based on 4 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

E15 on an older Hotpoint washer dryer or washing machine (the models with an Infobubble display, such as the WD72 and WDM73) means the water will not pump out. Hotpoint's own checks are that the drain hose is fitted correctly, the drain, standpipe or under-sink connection isn't blocked, and Rinse Hold isn't switched on. If all of that is fine, the usual culprit is a sock, hair clip or similar stuck in the sump hose or the pump.

What the code means

E15 is an E-series code from older Hotpoint machines, not the F-codes on Hotpoint's current UK list (on newer machines a drain fault shows as F05). The Hotpoint WD72/WDM73 washer dryer manual lists E15 as "Water will not pump out" and sends you to its "My machine does not pump out water (E15)" checks: has the drain hose been fitted correctly, are the drainage unit, standpipe or under-sink connection free from blockage, and has Rinse Hold been pressed (its light flashes until the button is pressed again). If the machine was just connected to a sink waste, Hotpoint also says to check the blanking end of the under-sink connection has actually been cut off. Hotpoint's WMA series service manual (as summarised by MyPros+) defines E15 as the machine failing to empty within the allowed time and starts the diagnosis at the pump, sump, pressure switch, pressure chamber and outlet hose. Owners of the WD72 report that the blockage is very often a sock, hair clips, a small plastic bag or a shoelace caught in the sump hose behind the machine or wrapped around the pump blades. Neighbouring codes in the same manual help narrow things down: E10, E11 and E13 are fill problems, E16 is a high water level, and E17 means the door isn't closed properly.

Likely causes

  • Rinse Hold has been pressed, so the machine is holding the water on purpose (Hotpoint's first check)
  • Drain hose fitted wrongly, kinked or pushed too far down the standpipe
  • Blocked standpipe, sink trap or under-sink spigot
  • New sink waste connection whose blanking end was never cut off
  • A sock, hair clip, plastic bag or shoelace blocking the sump hose or jamming the pump
  • Failed drain pump, or a pressure switch or pressure chamber fault (later steps in Hotpoint's service diagnosis)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Check whether Rinse Hold is on. If its light is flashing, press the button again and the machine should drain and carry on.
  2. Look behind the machine: make sure the drain hose isn't kinked or squashed and is fitted the way the installation section of your manual shows.
  3. Check the drain itself. Pour a jug of water down the standpipe or sink to see if it backs up, and clear the sink trap if it does. If the machine was just plumbed into a sink waste, make sure the blanking end of the spigot was cut off.
  4. Unplug the machine and empty the water before going further. Put towels down and use a shallow tray, since a full drum holds a lot of water.
  5. If your model has a pump filter behind a panel at the front, unscrew it slowly into the tray, clean it and check the pump impeller turns. On older models without a front filter, owners report the debris collects in the sump hose at the back or bottom of the machine, near the pump.
  6. Refit everything, plug in and run a spin or drain programme. If E15 comes back and you can't hear the pump running, or it only hums, the pump has probably failed.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • The hose, drain and sump are all clear and E15 keeps coming back, which points to the pump, the pressure switch or its pressure chamber.
  • Getting to the sump hose or pump means removing panels or tipping the machine, and you aren't comfortable doing that.
  • Water is leaking from the machine, not just staying in the drum.
  • You want the repair done by Hotpoint: book an engineer through Hotpoint's UK service line on 0344 822 4224.
