Code: E03🧺 Smeg Washer

Smeg Washing Machine E03 Error Code: Pump Fault or Blocked Pump Filter

Reviewed 2026-11-03 · Based on 4 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

On Smeg washing machines that use the E01 to E04 code table (such as the LBW50CIT), E03 means the drain pump is faulty, the pump filter is clogged, or the pump's electrical connection has failed, so the machine can't pump water out. Smeg's manual says to clean the pump filter first, and to contact an authorised service agent if the code comes back. Smeg UK lists E03 among the codes that need an engineer visit if it persists.

What the code means

Smeg's LBW50CIT user manual, section 10 "Automatic fault warnings and what to do", shows E03 as a combination of flashing wash-stage lights and gives the possible fault as "the pump is faulty or pump filter is clogged or electrical connection of the pump is faulty". The instruction is to clean the pump filter and, if the problem persists, contact the nearest authorised service agent. In the same table E01 is the door not shut properly, E02 is low water pressure or water level, and E04 is too much water in the machine. In practice E03 shows up as a drum still full of water at the end of a wash or rinse, or a machine that won't move on to spin. The manual's general troubleshooting table gives the same picture for "your machine does not discharge water": drain hose clogged or twisted, or pump filter clogged. Smeg UK's washing machine and washer dryer help page puts E03 in the group of codes that need an engineer visit. Not every Smeg uses this table: on the code list UK Whitegoods and Pacifica publish for many Smeg machines from about 2003 on, the drain fault is E6 and there is no E3 entry, so check the error table in your own manual (model number is on the label inside the door).

Likely causes

  • Pump filter clogged with lint, hair, coins, buttons or other small items (the most common cause, and the one Smeg tells you to check first)
  • Drain hose kinked, crushed, twisted or blocked behind the machine
  • Drain hose installed too high (Smeg says a maximum of 100 cm from the floor) or extended, which the manual says not to do
  • Blocked standpipe or sink trap the hose drains into
  • Faulty drain pump, such as a burnt-out motor or broken impeller
  • Loose or failed electrical connection to the drain pump

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Turn the machine off and unplug it before touching anything. If you've just run a hot wash, let the water cool first.
  2. Open the pump filter cover at the front lower right corner. Smeg's manual says you can lever it open with the powder spade or liquid detergent level plate supplied with the machine.
  3. Put a shallow container and towels under the filter first, because the water left in the drum will come out here. You may need to empty the container several times.
  4. Turn the filter anticlockwise and pull it out, let the water drain, then clean off any lint and foreign objects with a soft brush. Look into the opening and remove anything caught around the pump.
  5. Refit the filter by pushing it in and turning it clockwise until it's firm, and close the cover so its mountings line up with the holes in the front panel.
  6. Pull the machine out and check the drain hose along its whole length for kinks, crushing or blockage, and make sure the end is no higher than 100 cm from the floor and isn't extended. Clear the sink trap or standpipe if water backs up there.
  7. Plug back in and run a rinse and spin. If E03 comes back with a clean filter and a clear hose, stop there: Smeg's instruction is to contact an authorised service agent.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • E03 returns after cleaning the pump filter and checking the drain hose, which Smeg says needs an authorised service agent.
  • You can hear the pump humming but no water moves, or you hear nothing at all from the pump when the machine tries to drain, which points to the pump or its wiring.
  • The filter won't unscrew or is broken, since forcing it can crack the housing and cause a leak.
  • The machine is under warranty: Smeg Service UK lists 0330 094 7779 for in-warranty repairs, and Smeg also has a find-an-engineer service for out-of-warranty machines.
