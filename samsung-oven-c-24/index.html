Code: C-24♨️ Samsung Oven

Samsung Oven C-24 Error Code: Oven Heating Up Too Fast, Usually a Blocked Vent (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-10-23 · Based on 4 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

C-24 on a Samsung range or wall oven means the temperature sensor saw the oven heating up faster than normal. Samsung says this is usually because the oven vent on the back of the cooktop is blocked or partly blocked. Move anything covering the vent, then open and close the oven door a few times for about 3 seconds each to let hot air out, or turn the oven off and leave the door open for a minute. If C-24 keeps coming back with the vent clear, reset the power at the breaker for 30 seconds and request service if it still returns.

What the code means

Samsung's US range and wall oven error-code guide defines C-24 this way: the temperature sensor has detected that the oven's temperature is rising higher than the normal rate, and this is generally caused by the vent on the back of the cooktop being blocked or partly blocked. The oven pushes hot air out through that vent. When a pan, foil, a baking sheet or other object sits over it, heat builds up inside the cavity faster than the control expects, and it flags C-24. That makes C-24 different from its neighbors: C-21 means the oven actually got abnormally hot, and C-20 is an open or shorted oven sensor. In most cases C-24 is an airflow problem you can fix yourself, not a broken part. Codes and wording can vary by model and region, so check your own manual if your display doesn't match.

Likely causes

  • A pot, pan, griddle, baking sheet or foil sitting over the oven vent at the back of the cooktop (the cause Samsung names)
  • Grease or food residue partly clogging the vent opening
  • Heat building up during a long high-temperature bake or broil with the vent restricted
  • A temporary control glitch, if C-24 shows when the oven is cold and not in use
  • If C-24 returns with the vent clear: a fault in the temperature sensor, its wiring or the control, which needs a technician to diagnose

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before opening panels.

  1. Look at the vent on the back of the cooktop (on slide-in models it is usually at the rear, behind the burners). Move any pan, sheet or foil that is covering it.
  2. If you're mid-cook, clear the code by opening and closing the oven door a few times for about 3 seconds at a time. Samsung says this releases warm air and forces some out through the vent. Use oven mitts and stand back from the hot air.
  3. Or turn the oven off and leave the door open for about 1 minute to let it cool.
  4. Once the oven and cooktop are cool, wipe grease and crumbs out of the vent area with a damp cloth and mild soap. Never stuff foil or anything else into the vent.
  5. If C-24 or another code keeps coming back, turn the range off with its Power or Off button, then on again. If it still returns, turn the circuit breaker off, wait 30 seconds and turn it back on.
  6. Get in the habit of keeping the rear burner area clear of anything that overhangs the vent while the oven is running.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • C-24 returns after you've cleared the vent and reset the breaker. Samsung's guide says to request service at that point.
  • The code appears when the oven is cold or hasn't been used, even after a power reset.
  • The oven runs much hotter than the set temperature, keeps heating after you turn it off, or you smell burning plastic. Turn the breaker off and leave it off until it's checked.
  • The range is under warranty. Samsung's US warranty service goes through a Samsung authorized service center.
