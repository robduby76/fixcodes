Code: E30🧺 Bosch Washer

Bosch Washer E30 (E:30-10, E:30-20, E:30-80) Error Code: Water Supply, Too Much Foam or Not Draining (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-10-31 · Based on 7 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

On newer Bosch washing machines, E30 always comes with a second number, and that number tells you what's wrong. E:30 / -10 (often with a tap icon) is a water supply problem: the tap is off, the inlet hose is kinked, the inlet filter is blocked or the water pressure is low. E:30 / -20 means too much foam or extra water in the drum, usually from overdosing detergent. E:30 / -80 means the water isn't draining: a blocked drain pump, a kinked or blocked drain hose, a hose installed too high, or too much detergent. Most E30 faults can be fixed at home with the checks below.

What the code means

Bosch's newer washers show faults as a main code plus a sub-code, and the E:30 family covers the water level system: getting water in, foam in the drum, and getting water out. Bosch US and the Bosch owner's manuals list three versions. E:30 / -10, sometimes shown with a tap icon, means water isn't coming in properly: the tap is turned off, the inlet hose is kinked or jammed, the filters in the water inflow are blocked or the water pressure is low. Bosch also lists a defective water level measuring system as a cause; with this message the machine starts draining, so wait about 5 minutes, switch it off and on again, and call customer service if it comes back. Bosch France and the Netherlands treat E30-10 as the same fault as the older E17/F17 code (water fill time exceeded). E:30 / -20 is listed as a critical malfunction caused by too much detergent or by extra water being added during the cycle; the machine drains, and Bosch US also lists a defective solenoid (inlet) valve as a cause that needs service. E:30 / -80 means the soapy water isn't being pumped out, the same fault Bosch shows as E18, E36-10 or E36-25 on other models: the drain pipe or outlet hose is blocked or kinked, the outlet hose is installed higher than 1 metre (39 in) or has an unsuitable extension, the drain pump is blocked, the pump cap isn't screwed in fully, or there's too much detergent. Read the second number on your display before you start, because the fixes are different.

Likely causes

  • E:30 / -10: water tap turned off (Bosch US, Bosch manuals)
  • E:30 / -10: water inlet hose kinked or jammed, or the filters in the water inflow blocked (Bosch US, Bosch manuals)
  • E:30 / -10: low water pressure; Bosch Netherlands says the tap should deliver at least 10 litres (about one bucket) per minute
  • E:30 / -10: defective water level measuring system (Bosch US, Bosch manuals)
  • E:30 / -20: too much detergent, or extra water added to the machine while it was running; less often a defective solenoid valve (Bosch US, Bosch manuals)
  • E:30 / -80: drain pump blocked, or the pump cap not fitted correctly or not screwed in all the way (Bosch US, Bosch manuals)
  • E:30 / -80: drain pipe or outlet hose blocked or kinked, installed higher than 1 metre (39 in), or fitted with an unsuitable extension (Bosch US, Bosch manuals)
  • E:30 / -80: detergent dose too high, causing excess foam (Bosch US, Bosch manuals)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Check the full code on the display: -10 is water supply, -20 is too much foam or water, -80 is draining. Bosch says the machine starts a drain process with -10 and -20, so wait about 5 minutes for it to finish before you do anything.
  2. For -10: make sure the tap is fully open and the inlet hose isn't kinked or trapped behind the machine. If your water pressure is low, Bosch US says to contact a plumber.
  3. For -10: clean the inlet filter. Bosch Netherlands' method: turn the tap off, run any programme (not Spin or Drain) for about 40 seconds to release the pressure in the hose, stop it and unplug the machine, unscrew the hose from the tap and brush the filter clean. On Standard and Aqua-Secure models there's also a filter at the back of the machine, which you can pull out with pliers and clean. Reconnect the hose and check it doesn't leak.
  4. For -20 (and -80 with lots of foam): Bosch's immediate fix is to mix one tablespoon of fabric softener with 0.5 litres (about 2 cups) of water and pour it into compartment II. Don't do this when washing outdoor clothing, sportswear or down-filled items. Use less detergent next time with the same load, and don't add water to the machine while it's running.
  5. For -80: turn off the tap, switch off and unplug the machine, and let hot water cool first. Open the service flap at the bottom front, drain the water through the small emergency drain hose into a shallow container, then carefully unscrew the pump cover, clean out lint and objects, and check the pump impeller turns freely. Screw the cover back in fully with the handle vertical.
  6. For -80: check the drain hose isn't kinked or blocked, isn't installed higher than 1 metre (39 in), and has no unsuitable extension. Clean the drain pipe if water backs up there.
  7. Then switch the machine off and on to reset the fault. Bosch's general advice: if a code comes back, unplug it for at least 30 seconds and try again, then call Bosch Customer Support (US: 1-800-944-2904) and give them the exact code shown.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • E:30 / -10 keeps coming back even though the tap is on, the hose is clear and the filters are clean, which points to the water level measuring system or the inlet valve. Bosch says to call customer service if the message reappears.
  • E:30 / -20 appears when you are dosing detergent correctly and not adding water, since Bosch lists a defective solenoid valve as a cause.
  • E:30 / -80 persists after cleaning the pump and hose, or you can hear the pump hum without draining. Bosch France says a faulty drain pump can be the cause at that point.
  • The machine is under warranty, or you aren't comfortable draining and opening the pump area yourself.
