Code: F19🧺 Hotpoint Washer

Hotpoint Washer Dryer F19 Error Code: Fan Motor or Heating Fault

Reviewed 2026-10-26 · Based on 2 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

F19 on a Hotpoint washer dryer is a fan motor or heating fault in the drying system: the machine isn't drying, or it stops part way through the drying cycle. It only appears on washer dryers, not plain washing machines. Hotpoint's advice is to book an engineer, because the parts involved (the drying fan, drying heater and their wiring) sit inside the machine on mains voltage.

What the code means

Hotpoint UK's washer dryer error code guide lists F19 as "Fan motor or heating fault (washer dryers only)", with the symptom that the washer dryer is not drying or stops during the drying cycle, and its only advice is to call Hotpoint on 0344 822 4224 to book an engineer. Hotpoint's washing machine guide has no F19, because plain washers have no drying fan or drying heater. In other words, F19 sits in the drying circuit: a washer dryer dries by blowing air over a separate drying heater with a fan, and F19 means the electronics found a problem with that fan or that heating. Hotpoint doesn't publish a component-level test for F19, so the specific checks are for an engineer. The neighbouring drying codes on the same list help narrow it down: F13 is a dryer temperature sensing fault (the drying sensor) and F15 is a heater control fault that can stop drying or washing. If your display shows F19 and the machine also won't heat water on a wash, mention that to the engineer, as it points to a wider heating problem.

Likely causes

  • A failed or seized drying fan motor, so no air is moving over the drying heater (the fan side of Hotpoint's F19 definition)
  • A failed drying heater element or a tripped thermal cut-out on it (the heating side of the definition)
  • Loose, burnt or damaged wiring to the drying fan or drying heater
  • A fault on the main control board that drives the fan or heater

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Note when it happens: does F19 appear only on drying programmes, or also on wash-only programmes? That tells the engineer where to start.
  2. Run a wash-only programme. If the machine washes normally, you can keep using it to wash and air-dry clothes until it's repaired.
  3. Switch the machine off and unplug it for a couple of minutes, then try a drying programme again. This is general advice for clearing a one-off glitch, not a Hotpoint step for F19.
  4. Don't overload the drum on drying programmes and check the load is within the drying limit in your manual; this won't fix a failed part but avoids drying stalls being mistaken for faults.
  5. If F19 comes back, stop there and book an engineer: the fan and heater are inside the machine on mains voltage.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • F19 returns after a power reset: Hotpoint's only listed fix is an engineer visit.
  • You smell burning or see scorching near the top or back of the machine during drying: unplug it and don't use the drying function until it's checked.
  • The machine also won't heat on wash programmes, which suggests a wider heating or board fault.
  • The washer dryer is under warranty: call Hotpoint service on 0344 822 4224 rather than opening it up.
