20x25x5🌬 5-inch HVAC filter

20x25x5 Air Filter: Actual Size, MERV Guide & Replacement

Reviewed 2026-10-22 · 9 sources

⚡ Quick answer

A 20x25x5 filter is the nominal size, and three different actual sizes are sold under it. Honeywell, Lennox, Carrier and Bryant cabinets take about 19.88 x 24.75 x 4.38 inches, so match your cabinet before ordering, pick MERV 8–13, and check it monthly.

Nominal vs actual size

Nominal (order this)20 x 25 x 5 in
Actual19.88 x 24.75 x 4.38 in (Honeywell/Lennox/Carrier family; Filterbuy spec, two other cuts exist)

A 5-inch media-cabinet size, the boxed housing with a hinged door on the return duct of a central furnace, heat pump or AC air handler. One filter serves both heating and cooling. The size is set by the cabinet, which is often a different brand from the furnace, so read the cabinet's label or part number. Filters are deliberately cut smaller than their nominal label so they slide into the slot; length and width typically run about 1/4 to 1/2 inch under nominal. Actual sizes vary slightly by brand, so compare against the small print on your old frame.

How to measure

  1. Turn the system off at the thermostat (and the service switch) before pulling the filter.
  2. Read the frame first: the large print is the nominal size (what you order); small print, if present, is the actual size.
  3. If there is no label, measure length, width and depth edge to edge with a tape measure.
  4. Round each measurement up to the nearest whole inch to get the nominal size (e.g. 19.5 x 24.5 x 0.75 → 20x25x1).
  5. No old filter? Measure the filter slot or cabinet opening the same way.
  6. Note the airflow arrow: it should point toward the furnace or blower.

MERV 8 vs 11 vs 13

MERVMinimum capture (EPA / ASHRAE 52.2)Good for
8≥20% of 1–3 µm, ≥70% of 3–10 µmBasic dust, pollen, lint; the minimum EPA Indoor airPLUS requires
11≥20% of 0.3–1 µm, ≥65% of 1–3 µm, ≥85% of 3–10 µmA step up for pets and allergies
13≥50% of 0.3–1 µm, ≥85% of 1–3 µm, ≥90% of 3–10 µmEPA's recommended level for fine particles (PM2.5), if your system can accommodate it
⚠ When higher MERV can restrict airflow

EPA notes that higher-MERV filters tend to restrict airflow, and recommends using the highest rating your system fan and filter slot can accommodate. Most systems handle MERV 13 if the filter is replaced frequently. Thin 1-inch slots are the most restrictive; deeper 4-inch media filters generally create less static pressure. Weak airflow from vents, a system that runs longer, or a filter that bows are signs to step down a grade or ask an HVAC technician.

How often to replace

ENERGY STAR: check your filter every month and change it when it looks dirty, at least every three months. Pets, dust, renovations and heavy heating/cooling seasons can mean more frequent changes.

Equivalent & nearby sizes

FAQs

What is the actual size of a 20x25x5 air filter?

It depends on the cabinet. Filterbuy makes three cuts under the 20x25x5 name: 19.88 x 24.75 x 4.38 inches for Honeywell, Lennox, Carrier, Bryant, Payne and Day & Night cabinets; 20.13 x 24.13 x 4.88 for Trion Air Bear, GeneralAire, Skuttle, Ultravation and Air Kontrol; and 20.19 x 25.31 x 5.25 for Amana, Goodman, Coleman, Maytag, York, Electro-Air, Five Seasons, Nordyne and Totaline. Filterbuy says they don't interchange.

Which 20x25x5 do I need?

Go by the brand and part number on the filter cabinet, not the furnace; Filterbuy says it's on a sticker inside the cabinet door or on the housing. It lists Honeywell FC100A1037, FC200E1037 and FC35A1027, Carrier CF100A1025 and CF200A1016, and Lennox X6673 for the 4.38-inch cut; Trion 255649-102, 259112-102 and 229990-102 for the 4.88-inch cut; and FS2025, M8-1056, MU2025 and ABB2025 for the 5.25-inch cut.

No label and no old filter. How do I tell which one fits?

Measure height, width and depth to the nearest eighth of an inch and don't round up; Filterbuy says a wrong depth is the most common ordering mistake. A depth near 4 3/8 inches points to the Honeywell/Lennox/Carrier cut, about 4 7/8 to the Trion Air Bear cut, and about 5 1/4 to the Goodman/Amana cut. Turn the system off before opening the cabinet door.

Can I use a 20x25x4 in a 20x25x5 cabinet?

Usually not. Filterbuy says a 4-inch filter in a 5-inch cabinet leaves a gap so air goes around the filter instead of through it. EPA also says a filter must fit snugly so air doesn't leak around it.

How often should I replace a 20x25x5 filter?

Sources differ. Filterbuy's 20x25x5 page lists a 90-day service life for its MERV 8, 11 and 13 versions, while its general 5-inch filter page says six to twelve months. ENERGY STAR says to check your filter monthly, so look at the first one after a month or two and replace it when it looks dirty, sooner with pets, smoke or remodeling.

Is MERV 13 safe for my system?

EPA says most furnaces and HVAC systems can accommodate a MERV 13 filter without equipment problems if it is replaced frequently, but higher-MERV filters do tend to restrict airflow. A deep 5-inch filter has more media area than a thin one at the same rating. If you notice weak airflow or are unsure, ask an HVAC technician.

My filter says MPR or FPR, not MERV. What do I buy?

MPR (3M/Filtrete) and FPR (Home Depot) are proprietary scales, and EPA notes MERV is the national consensus standard, so conversions are approximate. Filterbuy maps roughly MPR 600 or FPR 5 to MERV 8, MPR 1000–1200 or FPR 7 to MERV 11, and MPR 1500–1900 or FPR 10 to MERV 13.

Which way does the arrow go?

Per ENERGY STAR, the airflow arrow on the filter frame should point toward the furnace or blower unit.
