Mitsubishi Mini Split Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Mitsubishi mini split code explained on FixCodes, 12 in all. Together they cite 12 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
E6Indoor and Outdoor Units Can't Communicate

P1Intake (Room Temperature) Sensor Error
P1 is the only one of Mitsubishi's three indoor thermistor codes that is about room air, not refrigerant pipe: it means the room temperature (intake) thermistor, TH1, is…
P2Pipe (TH2) Liquid Sensor Error
P2 on a Mitsubishi mini split means the liquid pipe thermistor, TH2, on the indoor unit's liquid refrigerant line is reading as shorted or open.
P4Float Switch or Drain Sensor Error
P4 on a Mitsubishi Electric mini split (Mr.
P5Drain Pump Error, Drain Overflow Protection
P5 on a Mitsubishi Electric indoor unit with a built-in drain pump (such as a ceiling cassette) means drain overflow protection has tripped.
P6Freezing or Overheating Protection
P6 on a Mitsubishi Electric indoor unit means freezing or overheating protection has tripped.
P8Pipe Temperature Error
P8 on a Mitsubishi Electric indoor unit is a pipe temperature error.
P9Pipe (TH5) Sensor Error
P9 on a Mitsubishi mini split means the condenser/evaporator pipe thermistor, TH5, which sits in a holder on the indoor coil piping, is reading as shorted or open.
PAForced Compressor Stop, Water Leakage Abnormality
