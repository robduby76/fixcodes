Code: H20🧺 Hotpoint Washer

Hotpoint Washer H20 Error Code: Not Filling With Water (Tap, Hose, Pressure or Inlet Filter)

Reviewed 2026-10-25 · Based on 4 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

H20 on a Hotpoint washing machine is a pun on H2O: the machine has failed to take in water. Hotpoint's instruction booklets say to check that the cold tap is turned fully on, the inlet hose is connected and not kinked, the house has water and enough pressure, and the small filter where the hose meets the machine isn't blocked. Most of the time it's a tap left off or a clogged inlet filter, and you can fix it in a few minutes. This code is on UK and European Hotpoint machines (Whirlpool EMEA), not the GE-made Hotpoint washers sold in the US.

What the code means

Hotpoint instruction booklets list H20 in the troubleshooting table under "The machine does not fill with water or 'H20' flashes in the display" (Hotpoint Extra WMXTF 842). The WT761 booklet says "Is H20 displayed on the control panel? This indicates the machine has failed to take in water", and the older Aquarius+ service section says the same: H20 flashing means the machine has failed to take in water (on plain Aquarius models without a display, a flashing Wash or Prewash light means the same thing). The causes Hotpoint lists are: the cold water tap isn't turned on, or not fully on; the inlet hose is kinked or not connected to the tap; the water pressure is too low; there is no water supply to the house; and the inlet valve filter is blocked. The WT761 booklet explains how to clean that filter: turn off the water, unscrew the fill hose at the back of the machine, and remove and clean the filter. The booklets also note that the Start button may not have been pressed properly (some models need it held for about 3 seconds), so make sure the cycle actually started. H20 isn't on Hotpoint UK's F-code list (F01 to F18), because it's a supply warning rather than a component fault. US Hotpoint washers are made by GE Appliances and use different codes.

Likely causes

  • Cold water tap turned off or only partly open (common after plumbing work or a new install)
  • Inlet hose kinked or crushed behind the machine, or not screwed onto the tap
  • Blocked inlet filter (the small mesh screen where the fill hose connects to the back of the machine)
  • Low water pressure or no water supply to the house (check another cold tap)
  • Frozen supply pipe or hose in an unheated garage or outbuilding
  • Faulty water inlet valve or its wiring (if the tap, hose, pressure and filter are all fine)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Check the cold tap that feeds the washer is turned fully on, and run a nearby cold tap to confirm the house has water and normal pressure.
  2. Pull the machine out a little and check the inlet hose along its length for kinks, and that it's screwed firmly onto the tap.
  3. Clean the inlet filter: turn the tap off, unplug the machine, put a towel down, and unscrew the fill hose from the back of the machine (a little water will come out). Pull out the mesh filter with pliers, rinse off any grit or scale, refit it and reconnect the hose hand-tight.
  4. While the hose is off, check the hose's own tap-end washer or filter (if it has one) for debris.
  5. If the pipe or hose could be frozen, let the area warm up before trying again.
  6. Turn the tap back on, check for leaks, plug in, select a programme and press Start (hold it for about 3 seconds on models that need it). Listen for water entering within a minute or so.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • The tap is fully on, pressure is normal, the hose and inlet filter are clear, and H20 still appears: the water inlet (fill) valve or its wiring probably needs testing.
  • You hear the valve click or buzz but no water comes in, or water keeps flowing in after the machine is switched off (switch off the tap and get it looked at).
  • The machine is under warranty: contact Hotpoint service (UK 0344 822 4224) rather than opening it up.
