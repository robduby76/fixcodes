Code: F03🧺 Hisense Washer

Hisense Washer F03 Error Code: Water Outlet (Drain) Fault, Washer Not Draining (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-11-08 · Based on 3 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

On Hisense washing machines, F03 is a drainage fault: the washer couldn't empty its water, so it stopped the programme. Hisense's manuals ask you to check the drain hose for kinks, blockages or freezing and to clean the drain pump filter, then restart. If F03 returns after that, the drain pump or its wiring needs a technician.

What the code means

Hisense's user manual for the HWF5I9014 front-loader lists F03 as "Water outlet fault" and tells you to check whether the drain hose is pinched and whether water is flowing in it, whether the drain hose filter is clogged, and whether the drain hose is frozen. After troubleshooting, press Start/Pause and then press Confirm Start within 60 seconds to continue. The older Hisense 3 Series manual describes the same code as "The washer is not emptied": the washer stops the programme, and you shut the machine down, disconnect the power and check for a knotted drain pipe, a blocked filter in the drain pump (let the water cool before draining) and a frozen drain hose. Then plug the machine back in, run the Spin programme, or hold Start/Pause for at least 3 seconds and restart the programme you want. A Spanish-language Hisense manual (WFMC6010E) shows the same drain fault, F03, with the drain tube and the pump or filter as the things to check. Hisense manuals group the codes this way: F01 is a water inlet fault, F03 drainage, F13 door lock, F14 door unlock and F24 overflow, and the HWF5I9014 manual treats other codes as an electronic module fault (the 3 Series manual gives that range as F04 to F98). If your washer fills but the water won't leave, F03 is the code to expect. If it won't fill at all, that's F01, not F03.

Likely causes

  • A kinked, pinched or squashed drain hose, or one that's blocked so no water flows through it (Hisense HWF5I9014 manual: check whether the drain hose is pinched and water is flowing)
  • A clogged drain hose filter or drain pump filter, often from lint, coins, buttons or hairpins (Hisense manual: if there is debris such as buttons, coins or hairpins in the drain pump, clean the pump filter)
  • A frozen drain hose in a cold garage, shed or unheated room (listed in both Hisense manuals)
  • A drain pump that has failed or isn't getting power, or a loose pump connection (general cause: the manuals send you to service when the checks above don't clear F03)
  • A blocked house drain or standpipe the hose empties into (general cause, not listed by Hisense for F03)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Turn the washer off and unplug it. Hisense's 3 Series manual says to shut down and disconnect the power supply before checking the drain.
  2. Check the drain hose from the back of the machine to where it empties. Straighten any kink or pinch, and check the hose isn't pushed too far down the standpipe or squashed behind the machine.
  3. Clean the drain pump filter, usually behind a small door at the bottom front. Lay towels and put a shallow container under it, because water will spill. Let the water cool before you drain anything. Turn the filter anticlockwise, pull it out, clear coins, buttons, hairpins and lint, and refit it fully so it doesn't leak. Hisense suggests cleaning it about every two months.
  4. If the washer sits somewhere that can freeze, warm the room and let the hose thaw before you try again.
  5. Plug the machine in and run the Spin programme. On the HWF5I9014, press Start/Pause and then Confirm Start within 60 seconds to continue. On the older 3 Series, hold Start/Pause for at least 3 seconds and restart the programme.
  6. If water is stuck in the drum and the door won't open, don't force it. Drain through the pump filter slowly, with the water cool, as your manual describes for emergency door opening.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • F03 comes back after the hose and pump filter are clear and the machine is restarted. Both Hisense manuals say to contact After-Sales Service.
  • The drain pump hums but doesn't move water, makes no sound at all, or the pump is leaking.
  • The pump filter housing is cracked or leaks after cleaning, or the drain hose is damaged inside the cabinet.
  • The machine is under warranty. Let Hisense service open it so you don't void the cover.
