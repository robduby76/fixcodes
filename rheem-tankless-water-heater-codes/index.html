Rheem Tankless Water Heater Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Rheem tankless water heater code explained on FixCodes, 11 in all. Together they cite 17 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
10Air Supply or Exhaust Blockage Warning
Code 10 on a Rheem tankless water heater is a warning, not a lockout: the heater isn't getting the air flow it expects through its venting.
11No Ignition, Flame Not Detected
Code 11 on a Rheem tankless gas water heater means the burner tried to light and the flame rod never sensed a flame.
12Flame Lit, Then Went Out
Code 12 on a Rheem tankless gas water heater means the burner lit but the flame went out during operation, several times in a row.
13Poor or Improper Combustion
Code 13 on a Rheem tankless water heater means the heater is burning, but not burning cleanly.
14Over Heat Limiter Active
Error 14 on a Rheem tankless water heater means the heater's overheat safety device has tripped.
17Water Leak Detected
Error code 17 on a Rheem tankless water heater means the water leak sensor inside the cabinet detected water.
29Condensate Not Draining
Code 29 on a Rheem high-efficiency (condensing) tankless water heater means condensate isn't draining out of the unit's built-in drain trap or neutralizer.
4545): Water Leak Detected
Er:45 on a Rheem condensing tankless water heater means its water leak detector has sensed water inside the cabinet.
61Blower Fan Speed Problem
Code 61 on a Rheem tankless water heater means the combustion blower (the fan that pushes air through the burner) isn't running at the right speed, or the control board…
76Display or Remote Controller Communication Failure
Error 76 on a Rheem tankless water heater means the control board has lost communication with the heater's built-in display or with a wired remote controller.
P1Not Enough Water Flow
P1 on a Rheem tankless water heater means there isn't enough water flowing through it to fire.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
