LG Oven Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every LG oven code explained on FixCodes, 6 in all. Together they cite 18 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
5b (Sb)Sabbath Mode, Not an Error (How to Turn It Off)
If your LG range or oven shows what looks like "5b," it is almost certainly "Sb" on the seven-segment display, and LG says Sb means Sabbath mode is on.
F3Touch Pad Key Short
F3 on an LG range or oven means the control thinks a button is being held down: a key on the touch pad, or the wiring to it, is reading as shorted.
F7Upper Convection Fan Error
F7 on most LG ranges and ovens means an upper convection fan error: the control isn't seeing the convection fan run.
F9Oven Not Heating During Preheat
F9 on an LG range means the oven isn't heating: the control called for heat during preheat and the temperature didn't climb.
F11Communication Error (or No Heating on Some Older Models)
On most LG ranges and wall ovens, F11 is a communication error: the main control board and another control board inside the range stopped talking to each other.
F19Lower Oven Not Heating
F19 on an LG range is the lower-oven version of F9: the lower oven was preheating and its temperature didn't climb the way the control expected.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
