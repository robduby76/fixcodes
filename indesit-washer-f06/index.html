Code: F06🧺 Indesit Washer

Indesit Washer F06 Error Code: Door Lock Fault (Door Won't Lock, Open or Start)

Reviewed 2026-10-26 · Based on 4 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

F06 on an Indesit washing machine is a door lock fault. Indesit says the door can't be opened or closed, or the machine won't start its programme once the door is shut. Check that nothing is caught in the door catch, switch off at the socket for a few minutes and try again, and if F06 comes back, book an engineer. Indesit also points owners to a safety recall on the door lock system of some Indesit and Hotpoint machines, so check your model.

What the code means

Indesit Service UK's washing machine error code guide lists F06 as a "Door lock fault". The symptoms it gives are that the door cannot be opened or closed, or that the programme will not start when the door is closed. Indesit's advice is to check for any obstructions in the door lock if the door will not close, and to call its service centre to book an engineer if the fault remains. The same entry carries a safety notice about a recall of the door lock system on certain Hotpoint and Indesit washing machines. Indesit's Arcadia service manual is more specific about what the board is seeing: the door lock failed to close or open, the door lock PTC triac (the switch on the main board that powers the lock) is stuck open or closed, or the board has lost its mains frequency or mains power signal. Its service actions are to look for water leaks that could short the contacts on the door lock connector, check the door lock terminal board for corrosion from manufacturing residues, check the door lock wiring, check the lock itself (about 170 ohms, plus or minus 10 percent, across its terminals on a classic door lock), and only then replace the door lock and, last, the main board. F06 is not a drain fault: if the door stays shut because water is still in the drum, that's F05. Hotpoint machines share Indesit's electronics and use F06 the same way.

Likely causes

  • Clothing, a towel corner or debris caught in the door catch so the door won't close fully
  • Broken door handle or latch hook, so the lock can't catch or release
  • Failed door lock (interlock) assembly
  • Water leak reaching the door lock connector, or a corroded door lock terminal board (service manual check)
  • Damaged or loose wiring between the door lock and the main board
  • Main control board fault, such as the door lock triac stuck open or closed (last, per the service manual)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Switch the machine off at the wall socket and unplug it. If the drum is full or the water is hot, don't force the door; drain it through the pump filter first.
  2. Leave the power off for about five minutes, then plug back in and try the door again. A power reset often releases a stuck lock.
  3. If the door won't close, check the catch and the hole it goes into for clothing, fluff or debris, and make sure the door hook isn't bent or broken.
  4. If the door is stuck shut, many Indesit and Hotpoint models have an emergency release: with the machine unplugged, pop off the lower front panel and look near the pump filter for a cord or tab (often green) and pull it down while opening the door.
  5. Look under and around the machine for signs of a leak, since water reaching the door lock wiring can trigger F06.
  6. Check whether your machine is covered by the Hotpoint and Indesit door lock recall using its model and serial number. If it is, stop using it and follow the recall instructions.
  7. Plug back in, close the door firmly until it clicks, and start a short programme to test.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • F06 comes back after a power reset and the catch is clear: Indesit recommends booking an engineer.
  • The machine is on the door lock recall list, or you smell burning near the door: unplug it and contact Indesit or the recall line.
  • The door lock needs testing or replacing, or there's water near the wiring: this means working on live electrical parts behind the front panel.
  • A new door lock didn't fix it, which points to the main board.
