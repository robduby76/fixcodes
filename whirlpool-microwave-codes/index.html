Whirlpool Microwave Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Whirlpool microwave code explained on FixCodes, 6 in all. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
F1E4Control Board or Microwave Relay Fault
F1E4 on a Whirlpool over-the-range microwave means the control could not carry out a function, and Whirlpool points to the appliance control board (ACU) or its wiring.
F2Keypad Fault (Over-the-Range) or Oven Temperature Sensor (Built-In)
F2 on a Whirlpool microwave means different things depending on the type.
F6Microwave Relay or Relay Control Board Fault
F6 on a Whirlpool over-the-range microwave means the control could not carry out a function, and Whirlpool points to the relay control board or its wiring.
