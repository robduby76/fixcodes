Code: E24🌀 Bosch Dryer

Bosch Dryer E24 Error Code: Electronic Fault (Heat Pump Inverter or Software Mismatch)

Reviewed 2026-10-26 · Based on 5 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

E24 on a Bosch dryer is an electronics fault that Bosch says only an authorized technician can fix. On heat pump dryers, repair sources tie it to a communication fault between the main electronics and the compressor's inverter board. On some older US WTMC/WTXD models, Bosch training material describes it as a software version mismatch that stops the dryer. Try a power reset of at least 5 minutes once; if E24 comes back, book Bosch service with your model number (E-Nr).

What the code means

Bosch's US dryer error code guide groups E24 with E06, E08, E09, E25, E28, E36 and E90 under one heading: there is an electronic fault, and it can only be fixed by an authorized technician. Bosch's Middle East (Qatar) support page for E24 says the same: a fault with the electronics that only an experienced engineer can eliminate. What the electronics fault is depends on the dryer. A German service technician fault table for Bosch heat pump dryers lists E24 as a compressor/inverter communication error: the DBUS link to the inverter is broken, the inverter isn't switched on, or the inverter is defective. German parts retailer ersatzteilshop.de also lists the heat pump compressor and the compressor's inverter electronics as the parts involved. For older US WTMC/WTXD dryers, the technician reference MyPros+ quotes the Bosch training manual as saying E:24 means the software version doesn't match its table, and the dryer stops and can't be restarted. Either way it's a board-level fault, not something a lint or vent cleaning will fix. If your dryer has no heat pump (most US vented models), the heat pump inverter meaning doesn't apply, which is why your E-Nr matters to the technician. See also the related Bosch electronic fault page for E90.

Likely causes

  • On heat pump dryers: a broken or loose DBUS cable between the main electronics and the compressor inverter (German service technician fault table)
  • On heat pump dryers: the inverter not powering up, or a failed inverter board (German service technician fault table; ersatzteilshop.de)
  • On heat pump dryers: a fault in the heat pump compressor itself (ersatzteilshop.de)
  • On some older US WTMC/WTXD models: control software that doesn't match the dryer's parameter table, for example after a board replacement (Bosch WTMC/WTXD training manual via MyPros+)
  • A general electronic fault, which Bosch says needs an authorized technician (Bosch US; Bosch Middle East)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Turn off power at the furnace/HVAC service switch and the breaker before opening panels.

  1. Write down the exact code and your model number (E-Nr) from the rating label inside the door.
  2. Power reset: unplug the dryer or switch off its breaker for at least 5 minutes, then power it back on and try a short program.
  3. If the dryer had a board replaced recently, tell the technician who did it, since a software mismatch can show up as E24 on some models.
  4. If E24 returns, stop there. The inverter and control boards carry line voltage, and Bosch says this code needs a technician.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • E24 comes back after a power reset: call Bosch Customer Support at 1-800-944-2904 (US) or book an authorized technician, and give them your E-Nr.
  • On a heat pump dryer, ask them to check the cable between the main board and the compressor inverter and whether the inverter powers up when a program starts, before replacing parts.
  • If a control board was replaced, the technician should check that its software and coding match your dryer.
