Trane Furnace Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Trane furnace code explained on FixCodes, 11 in all. Together they cite 13 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
1DL / IdLIdle, Not an Error
1DL (also shown as IdL or 1dL) on a Trane furnace's seven-segment display is not a fault.
2 flashesSystem Lockout, Ignition Retries Exceeded
A red LED blinking 2 times then pausing on a Trane (or American Standard) furnace control board means the furnace locked out after too many failed ignition attempts.
3 flashesDraft Pressure Switch Error
A red LED blinking 3 times then pausing on a Trane (or American Standard) furnace control board is a draft pressure error.
4 flashesOpen Temperature Limit Circuit
A red LED blinking 4 times then pausing on a Trane (or American Standard) furnace control board means the temperature limit circuit is open.
5 flashesFlame Sensed When No Flame Should Be Present
A red LED blinking 5 times then pausing on a Trane (or American Standard) furnace control board means the control senses a flame when there should not be one.
6 flashes115 VAC Power Reversed, Poor Grounding or Low Voltage
On older Trane and American Standard furnaces with an integrated furnace control (IFC), a red LED blinking 6 times then pausing means the control sees 115 volt AC power…
7 flashesGas Valve Circuit Error
On Trane and American Standard furnaces with an integrated furnace control (IFC), a red LED blinking 7 times then pausing means a gas valve circuit error.
8 flashesLow Flame Sense Signal
On Trane and American Standard furnaces with an integrated furnace control (IFC), a red LED blinking 8 times then pausing means a low flame sense signal.
9 flashesIgnitor Circuit or Grounding Problem
On many Trane and American Standard furnaces with an integrated furnace control (IFC), a red LED blinking 9 times then pausing points to the hot surface ignitor circuit.
ARFAirflow Readout, Not an Error
ARF on the three-digit display of a Trane furnace control board is not an error code.
COFContinuous Fan Readout, Not an Error
COF on the three-digit display of a Trane furnace control board is not an error code.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
