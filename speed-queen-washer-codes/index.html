Speed Queen Washer Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Speed Queen washer code explained on FixCodes, 5 in all. Together they cite 8 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
drWasher Didn't Drain
Er, dr (also shown as E dr or E:dr) on a Speed Queen washer is a drain error: water is still in the tub after the washer tried to pump it out.
EdDrive Board Errors
Ed on a Speed Queen front-load washer means the motor drive board has reported a fault, and the number after it (shown as Ed, 29 or E d 29) tells you which one.
FLFill Error, Water Level Not Reached
FL on a Speed Queen washer shows as Er, FL and means a fill error: the washer didn't reach its fill level within 30 minutes.
L1Lid Did Not Lock
L1 on a Speed Queen top-load washer is the Er, Ll code, and it means the lid didn't lock when the washer tried to lock it.
nFNo Water Flow
Er, nF on a Speed Queen washer means the washer sensed no water, or almost no rise in the water level, within 5 minutes of starting a fill.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
