Code: F5 E3🧺 Whirlpool Washer

Whirlpool Washer F5 E3: Door Lock Error, Door Won't Lock or Unlock (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-10-22 · Based on 5 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

F5 E3 on a Whirlpool front-load washer is a door lock error. Whirlpool's owner help says the door isn't locking properly, and its service tech sheet calls F5E3 a door unlock error: the washer tried to unlock the door 6 times and couldn't. Start by making sure the door is fully closed with nothing caught in it, then reset the washer by unplugging it for 5 minutes. If it keeps coming back, the door lock (door switch/lock assembly) is the usual suspect.

What the code means

Whirlpool's Product Help article for F5 E3 on front-load washers says the code indicates the door is not locking properly, and tells owners to make sure the door is completely closed with nothing blocking the latch, then reset the control by cutting power for 5 minutes. Whirlpool's technician tech sheet (W10254474B) is more specific: F5E3 is a door unlock error, set when the washer attempts to unlock the door 6 times and can't, so it goes into pause and stores the code. Its listed causes are a door latch that isn't secured to the front panel, or a misaligned, broken or over-tightened latch, checked with the door lock system test. Related codes on the same washers are F5 E1 (door switch) and F5 E2 (door won't lock). On Whirlpool Laundry Tower models, Whirlpool lists F5E3 under the door switch with the same close-the-door-and-reset steps. Whirlpool's top-load help also has an F5E3 article whose only step is the 5-minute power reset; on top-loaders it relates to the lid lock.

Likely causes

  • Door not fully closed: clothing, a sock or the door seal caught in the opening
  • Overloaded drum pushing against the door so it can't latch or release cleanly
  • Door latch (the hook on the door) loose, misaligned, broken, or over-tightened against the front panel
  • A worn or failed door switch/lock assembly that can't lock or release
  • A temporary control glitch that clears after a power reset
  • Less often, a wiring or main control (CCU) problem found during the door lock system test

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Open the door if it will open, check for laundry or the gasket caught in the opening, and close it firmly. Whirlpool notes the door can look closed without being latched.
  2. If the load is packed tight, take some out so the clothes can tumble freely, then close the door again.
  3. Reset the washer: unplug it (or switch off its breaker) for 5 minutes, plug it back in, and start a cycle. This is Whirlpool's first step for F5 E3.
  4. Look at the latch hook on the door. If it's loose, bent, cracked or sits out of line with the lock opening, it needs tightening or replacing (don't over-tighten it; the tech sheet lists that as a cause).
  5. If the door stays locked with wet clothes inside, make sure the drum has drained first (a full drum will flood out). Whirlpool's tech sheet has a manual release: with power off, remove the top panel, reach down behind the front to the top of the door switch/lock assembly, and gently pull the ring-shaped tab up about 1/4 in until it clicks. Only do this if you're comfortable removing panels.
  6. If F5 E3 comes back after the door is clear and the washer has been reset, plan on replacing the door lock assembly or booking service.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • The code returns after a reset and the door and latch look fine.
  • The door is locked shut with water in the drum and it won't drain.
  • You'd need to test the door lock circuit with a meter. Whirlpool's door lock system test involves live voltage checks.
  • The new door lock didn't fix it, which points to wiring or the control board.
  • The washer is under warranty. Schedule Whirlpool service instead of opening it.
