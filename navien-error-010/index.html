Navien Error Code 10 (E010 / 10E): Abnormal Air Pressure on Older Navien Units (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-10-23 · Based on 4 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

Navien error code 10 (E010, shown as 10E on some controllers) means abnormal air pressure on older Navien units, such as the NR/NP series tankless water heaters and the CH-180/CH-240 combi boilers. The unit isn't moving the air through the burner that it expects. Navien's own error list says to check the vent and fresh-air pipe for obstructions, clean the air filter, and check the hoses and condensate drain. Newer Navien models (NPE, NPE-2, NCB-E) report the same problem as E110 instead.

What the code means

Navien's NR/NP series service manual (version 2.0, 2010) and the CH-180 service manual both list E010 as Abnormal air pressure. The NR/NP manual explains how the control decides this. During pre-purge, if the fan is turning but the air volume (air pressure) sensor voltage never reaches the level needed for ignition, the unit retries pre-purge; after 10 failed pre-purges in a row it declares an air pressure error (or an air pressure hose error) and locks out. During combustion, if the fan is running 1,000 RPM or more above its standard speed, the control treats that as an air pressure error, stops burning and keeps the fan running, then cancels the error once the fan speed comes back to within 500 RPM of standard. In plain terms, something is restricting air in or out of the unit, the small hose to the air pressure sensor is damaged or holding water, or the sensor or board has failed. If you have a current NPE or NCB-E model, its manuals call this E110; see our Navien E110 page.

Likely causes

  • A blocked vent or fresh-air (intake) pipe, for example snow, leaves, a nest or debris at the outside termination. This is the first check on Navien's E010 list.
  • A dirty air filter. Navien's error list says to check and clean it.
  • A torn, holed or water-filled air pressure hose. The NR/NP service manual says to inspect it for tears and holes and for water left in the hose after installation.
  • A clogged condensate hose or drain, which can back water up into the vent
  • Rainwater collected in the air supply or exhaust pipe because of a dent or a pipe installed sloping the wrong way
  • Exhaust gas getting pulled back into the air supply. The service manual notes this inflow of exhaust gas makes noise.
  • A clogged venturi (burner) hole
  • A defective air pressure sensor or control board (PCB)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Turn off power at the furnace/HVAC service switch and the breaker before opening panels. Shut the gas valve before any work near the burners, and never disconnect or alter the gas line. If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility.

  1. Turn the unit off and disconnect power before you open the cover.
  2. Go outside and look at both vent terminations, the exhaust and the fresh-air intake. Clear away snow, ice, leaves, nests or anything blocking them.
  3. Look along any vent pipe you can see for dents, sags or spots where rainwater could sit, and note them for your installer.
  4. Check and clean the air filter, as Navien's error list says. On these older units the filter is behind the front cover.
  5. Check that the condensate drain hose isn't kinked, frozen or clogged and that it drains freely.
  6. Restore power and try hot water or heat again. If error 10 comes back after the vents, filter and drain are clear, stop resetting it and call for service.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • Error 10 keeps coming back after the vents, air filter and condensate drain have been checked
  • Inspecting and replacing the air pressure sensor hose, and clearing water out of it
  • Checking the venturi (burner) hole, the air pressure sensor and the PCB. The NR/NP service manual checks the sensor's signal voltage and the fan's operating voltage as part of its 10E flowchart.
  • Fixing vent slope, dents, length or termination problems, or exhaust recirculating into the intake. Venting on a gas appliance should be corrected by a licensed installer.
  • The manual notes that if the error happens only now and then on some older units, Navien's fix was a replacement controller. That is a technician call.
