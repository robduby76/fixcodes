Code: H🧺 Hotpoint Dishwasher

Hotpoint Dishwasher "H" on the Display: No Water Coming In (Tap Shut-Off Alarm)

Reviewed 2026-10-26 · Based on 4 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

An H on a Hotpoint dishwasher display, usually with beeping, means the machine isn't detecting water coming in. Hotpoint's own troubleshooting table says the dishwasher does not fill, the display shows H then 6, and the On/Off light blinks rapidly; the causes it lists are no water in the supply or the tap shut, a bent inlet hose, or a clogged sieve in the inlet hose. Open the tap fully, straighten the hose, clean the hose sieve, then switch off, back on and restart a program. If H keeps coming back with the tap on, the inlet valve or the flow meter (turbine) is the usual suspect and an engineer should check it. This applies to UK and European Hotpoint dishwashers (Whirlpool EMEA, formerly Indesit Company), not the GE-made Hotpoint dishwashers sold in the US.

What the code means

Hotpoint's quick guide for its current dishwashers (for example the H2IHD526BUK) lists "The dishwasher does not fill the water. Display shows: H, 6 and On/Off LED is blinking rapidly", with the causes no water in the water supply or the tap is closed, the inlet hose is bent, or the sieve in the water inlet hose is clogged. In practice H is the early warning: the board has opened the fill valve but isn't counting any water going in, so it beeps and shows H for a few minutes while it keeps trying. If nothing changes, it gives up and the display moves to 6, which is the same fault Hotpoint UK lists as F06, water fill timeout (on LED-only models, LED 7 and 8 on), with the advice to check the water supply is present and at sufficient pressure, the tap is fully on and the fill hose is not restricted. Hotpoint UK's fault explanation for the fill timeout adds a low water pressure or a flow meter that doesn't work, and a faulty or disconnected water solenoid valve. An Indesit/Hotpoint dishwasher service manual describes the same sequence from the engineer's side: during the static fill the main module gets no signal from the litre-count turbine, so after a short time the machine beeps and shows H, and after a further period it times out with fault 06. Older Hotpoint models such as the FDW80 show "H20" for the same tap-off condition and switch to AL06 if nobody responds. Don't confuse H with F07 (water turbine damaged), where water actually got in but the turbine never reported it, or with 15 / F15, a separate code covered on its own page.

Likely causes

  • The water tap (isolating valve) to the dishwasher is off or only partly open, or there's no water in the supply, the first cause Hotpoint lists
  • The inlet hose is bent, kinked or squashed behind the machine
  • The sieve (filter) in the water inlet hose is clogged with scale or debris
  • Water pressure is too low, for example while other taps or appliances are running (Hotpoint UK lists low pressure for the fill timeout)
  • A faulty or disconnected water inlet (solenoid) valve, which Hotpoint UK lists for the fill timeout
  • A worn or faulty flow meter (turbine) in the air-break unit on the side of the machine, so water goes in but the board doesn't count it (Hotpoint UK lists a flow meter that doesn't work; the service manual describes the turbine signal)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Check the tap that feeds the dishwasher (usually under the sink) is turned fully on, and that other taps in the house have normal flow.
  2. Pull the machine out a little if needed and make sure the inlet hose isn't bent, kinked or trapped behind the dishwasher.
  3. Turn the tap off, unscrew the inlet hose from the tap and clean the small sieve (filter) in the hose end under running water, then refit it hand-tight and turn the tap back on.
  4. Switch the dishwasher off with the On/Off button, wait a few seconds, switch it back on and restart the program, as Hotpoint advises. If the machine locked out because nobody answered the beeps, this off-and-on step is what clears it.
  5. If H comes back with the tap fully on and a clean, straight hose, stop there and book an engineer: the inlet valve or the flow meter (turbine) needs testing, and both sit behind the side panel next to mains wiring.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • H or 6 / F06 keeps returning even though the tap is fully on and the hose sieve is clean: the inlet valve or flow meter needs testing. Call Hotpoint on 0344 822 4224 or book a qualified engineer.
  • The machine fills (you can hear water) but still beeps and shows H, or it overfills: that points to the flow meter (turbine) not counting water, which an engineer can confirm.
  • Water pressure in the whole house is low: a plumber should check the supply before the dishwasher is blamed.
  • The machine is under warranty: use Hotpoint's own service so the repair stays covered.
