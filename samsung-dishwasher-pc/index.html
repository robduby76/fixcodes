Samsung Dishwasher PC (PE) Error Code: Zone Wash / Dividing Valve Problem (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-10-23 · Based on 5 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

PC on a Samsung dishwasher means the Zone Wash function isn't working correctly. Behind the scenes, Samsung's other support pages tie PC (and its twin PE) to the dividing valve, the part that sends water to different spray zones. Turn the dishwasher off at the breaker for at least 30 seconds, then start a Normal cycle without Zone Wash or Half Load selected. If PC comes back, Samsung says to request service.

What the code means

Samsung US and Samsung Canada call PC a "Zone Wash error": the Zone Wash function is not operating correctly. On those support pages PE is the related "Half load function error." Samsung UK, Australia and the Gulf group PC and PE together as a dividing valve check, displayed when the dividing valve is defective, and the Gulf page explains that this component controls water distribution within the dishwasher. Zone Wash and Half Load both work by steering water to only part of the dishwasher, so a valve that can't switch position stops those options. Samsung UK describes the symptom as a dishwasher that powers on but won't start a cycle or stops shortly after starting, often when Zone Wash or Half Load has been selected.

Likely causes

  • A temporary control glitch after Zone Wash or Half Load was selected (a power reset often clears it)
  • A defective dividing valve, the part that directs water to different spray zones (Samsung UK, Australia and Gulf)
  • A fault in the Zone Wash or Half Load function itself (Samsung US and Canada)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Turn the dishwasher off, then switch off its circuit breaker (or unplug it) for at least 30 seconds. Samsung UK gives 30 seconds as the minimum.
  2. Restore power, make sure Zone Wash and Half Load are NOT selected, load the dishwasher normally and start a Normal cycle.
  3. If you see PE instead, Samsung US says to empty the dishwasher, add detergent and run a half load cycle to check the function.
  4. If the Normal cycle runs fine, you can keep using the dishwasher without Zone Wash or Half Load until it's serviced, but tell the technician about the code.
  5. If PC shows up again, switch the dishwasher off at the breaker and request service. Samsung doesn't list any further owner checks for this code.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • PC comes back after a 30-second power reset and a Normal cycle. Samsung US and Canada say to request service at that point.
  • The dishwasher won't run any cycle at all, even with Zone Wash and Half Load turned off.
  • The dividing valve needs testing or replacing. It sits under the tub, so the dishwasher has to be pulled out and opened up.
  • The dishwasher is under warranty. Book Samsung service rather than replacing parts yourself.
