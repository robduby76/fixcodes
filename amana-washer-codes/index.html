Amana Washer Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Amana washer code explained on FixCodes, 4 in all. Together they cite 12 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
5dToo Many Suds
5d on an Amana washer is really Sd, short for excess suds (some models show Sud).
F8 E1Not Filling Fast Enough
F8 E1 on an Amana washer means the washer isn't detecting the right amount of incoming water, so it's taking too long to fill.
F9 E1Long Drain Time
F9 E1 on an Amana washer means Long Drain: the washer took too long to pump the water out, typically more than 8 minutes.
ndNo Drain, Water Won't Pump Out
nd on an Amana front-load washer means No Drain: the water level didn't drop below the low water reset level within 15 minutes before a spin.

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
