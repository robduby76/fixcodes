Code: EF🧊 GE Refrigerator

GE Refrigerator EF Code: Usually TurboFreeze (tF), Not an Error (Meaning, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-10-22 · Based on 4 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

On a GE refrigerator, "EF" in the freezer temperature display is almost always the TurboFreeze indicator "tF", which GE says can look like an EF or an upside-down F with an F. It is not a fault. It means the freezer is running at its coldest setting for up to 2 1/2 hours. Press and hold the Freezer pad for 3 seconds (or press Express Mode on touch-screen models) to turn it off, or just let it time out.

What the code means

GE Appliances' own refrigerator code guide lists EF under normal function codes, not fault codes: on models with TurboFreeze, the tF setting indicator can look like an EF, an upside-down F with an F, or something similar. GE's TurboFreeze article explains what is happening while it shows: the compressor is activated and the freezer fan runs at high speed, even with the door open, and the freezer runs at its coldest setting for 2 1/2 hours or until TurboFreeze is turned off, whichever comes first. The fresh food side has the same kind of indicator: tC for TurboCool can look like an EC. Many repair sites describe a combined "SY EF" code as an evaporator fan failure. That pairing appears in Frigidaire and Electrolux service data sheets; we could not find it in any GE Appliances document, so don't assume it applies to a GE refrigerator. If your GE shows EF and the freezer is actually warming up, treat it as a cooling problem: GE's technical service guide lists a failed evaporator fan, a failed condenser fan, a thermistor fault or a control board fault among the causes of a warm freezer.

Likely causes

  • TurboFreeze was turned on, often by holding the Freezer pad for 3 seconds by accident or by pressing Express Mode on touch-screen models
  • Someone turned on TurboFreeze after a big grocery load and it is still inside its 2 1/2 hour window
  • The display font makes the tF indicator read as EF (GE notes it can look like an upside-down F with an F)
  • If the freezer is also warm: a stopped or iced-up evaporator fan, a failed condenser fan, a thermistor fault or a main control board fault (per GE's technical service guide)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Check whether TurboFreeze is on. Press and hold the Freezer pad for 3 seconds to toggle it, or press Express Mode on models with a touch-screen (LCD) display. GE says the same press turns it on or off.
  2. If you just loaded a lot of warm food, it's fine to leave TurboFreeze running. GE says it shuts off by itself after 2 1/2 hours.
  3. Expect the freezer fan to sound louder than normal and keep running with the door open while TurboFreeze is on. GE says the fan runs at high speed during TurboFreeze.
  4. If the code isn't TurboFreeze or won't go away, disconnect power for a minute or two and plug it back in. GE says that sometimes resets the control panel and clears a displayed code.
  5. Put an appliance thermometer in the freezer. If it stays well above normal freezer temperature and you can't hear the fan behind the freezer back panel, the fan or its control may have a problem rather than a display setting.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • The freezer or fresh food section is warming up, whatever the display says.
  • You can't hear the freezer fan running when the compressor is on, or you hear the fan scraping on ice. GE's technical service guide covers fan voltage checks that need the back panel removed.
  • The code returns right after a power reset and TurboFreeze is off.
  • Other codes appear, such as dE (defrost system has not operated properly in the last 24 hours) or FF (frozen food may be thawing), which GE lists as fault codes.
  • The refrigerator is under warranty. Schedule GE service with your model number rather than opening the freezer panel.
