P0701: Transmission Control System Range/Performance (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-10-25 · Based on 2 cited source(s)

⚡ Quick answer

P0701 means the engine or transmission computer has found a performance problem in the automatic transmission's control system. OBD-Codes.com says it is usually stored only when other transmission codes are present, and those should be diagnosed and fixed first. Low or bad fluid is a leading cause.

🚨 Safety first

A flashing check engine light means a severe misfire: slow down and stop driving soon. A red brake or oil pressure warning needs immediate attention. Pull over safely and don't keep driving.

What P0701 means

Automatic transmissions on OBD-II vehicles are run electronically: a control module reads engine inputs (throttle angle, RPM, load, coolant temperature) and transmission inputs (pump pressure, input and output speed, shifter position, fluid temperature) and operates shift, pressure-control and torque converter clutch solenoids. OBD-Codes.com explains that most vehicles build the transmission controller into the powertrain control module (PCM), while some use a separate transmission control module (TCM) that shares data over the CAN network. It says P0701 is a generic code (it lists Ford, Honda, Mazda, Mercedes and VW among examples) that sets when the PCM detects a malfunction in the transmission control system, that it applies only to automatic transmissions, and that it is unusual because it is usually only stored when other transmission codes, mechanical or electrical, are also present. The sources describe it differently: RepairPal says the PCM sets P0701 when the TCM cannot select and engage the correct gear, using the same wording it uses for P0702, while OBD-Codes.com treats it as a general range/performance fault whose causes run from low fluid to internal mechanical failure. Related codes are P0700 (transmission control system MIL request), P0702 (transmission control system electrical) and P0703 (brake switch B circuit).

Severity: can I keep driving?

Stop and fix promptly

Get it fixed right away. OBD-Codes.com says a vehicle with P0701 should be diagnosed immediately because the limp-in mode it can trigger is not meant for long-term use. RepairPal rates P0701 Urgent, meaning it can cause severe damage to the vehicle. Avoid long drives and highway speeds until it's diagnosed.

Common symptoms

  • Check engine light on (OBD-Codes.com)
  • Harsher than normal shifts if the transmission goes into limp-in mode, which greatly raises pump pressure (OBD-Codes.com)
  • Erratic shift patterns or the transmission not shifting properly (both sources)
  • Transmission slipping or delayed engagement (OBD-Codes.com)
  • Decreased fuel efficiency or increased fuel consumption (both sources)
  • Other transmission-related codes stored at the same time (OBD-Codes.com)

Likely causes

Roughly most to least common, based on the sources below. Your vehicle may differ.

  1. Low transmission fluid level (OBD-Codes.com)
  2. Substandard or wrong transmission fluid (OBD-Codes.com)
  3. Failed transmission sensor (OBD-Codes.com)
  4. Open or shorted circuits in the transmission control system (OBD-Codes.com)
  5. Internal mechanical transmission failure or torque converter defects (OBD-Codes.com)
  6. Defective transmission controller or a PCM programming error (OBD-Codes.com)
  7. RepairPal's description: the TCM cannot select and engage the correct gear (RepairPal)

Diagnostic steps you can do

⚠ Before you start

Work on a cool engine, parked on level ground with the parking brake set. Never work under a car supported only by a jack.

  1. Read and write down all stored codes and freeze frame data before clearing anything. OBD-Codes.com says to diagnose and repair any other transmission codes first, starting with the one stored first, before working on P0701.
  2. Check for technical service bulletins (TSBs) for your vehicle, since the problem may be a known issue with a known fix.
  3. Check the transmission fluid level and condition the way your owner's manual specifies. OBD-Codes.com says fluid level and condition are the leading cause of transmission failure, and a strong burnt smell points to serious internal damage.
  4. If the fluid is more than a quart low, top it up with the specified fluid and look for leaks at the transmission, cooler and cooler lines, then test drive to see if the code returns.
  5. Visually inspect the transmission wiring and connectors, and test the battery, cables and terminal ends, as OBD-Codes.com recommends.
  6. Use a service manual, scan tool, digital volt/ohmmeter and a transmission pressure gauge for deeper testing, since procedures vary by make and model.

Typical fixes

  • Repair the faults behind any other stored transmission codes
  • Correct the fluid level, repair leaks, or service the fluid with the correct type
  • Repair open or shorted wiring or connectors, or replace a failed transmission sensor
  • Repair internal transmission or torque converter damage if testing confirms it
  • Reprogram or replace the PCM or TCM only after fluid, wiring, sensors and other codes check out

Repair costs vary widely by vehicle, region, parts brand and shop, so we don't quote prices. Get a written estimate after diagnosis.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • The transmission slips, won't shift, is stuck in limp mode, or shifts very harshly
  • The fluid smells burnt, which OBD-Codes.com links to internal failure
  • Several transmission codes are stored and you don't have a scan tool or wiring diagrams
  • Pressure testing, internal repair or module reprogramming is needed

FAQs

Is P0701 serious?

Yes. RepairPal rates it Urgent, and OBD-Codes.com says it should be diagnosed immediately because limp-in mode isn't meant for long-term use.

Can low transmission fluid cause P0701?

Yes. OBD-Codes.com lists low fluid first among the causes and says fluid level and condition are the leading cause of transmission failure, so check the fluid before anything else.

Why do I have other transmission codes with P0701?

OBD-Codes.com says P0701 is usually only stored when other transmission codes are present and recommends fixing those first, starting with the one that was stored first.

How much does it cost to fix P0701?

Neither source gives a repair cost. It ranges from a fluid top-up to internal transmission work, so get a diagnosis or written estimate first.
