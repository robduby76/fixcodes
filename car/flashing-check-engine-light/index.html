Flashing check engine light: what it means and what to do

⚡ Quick answer

A flashing check engine light means a severe engine misfire. Reduce speed and stop driving soon. Severe misfire can damage the catalytic converter in a short time.

🚨 Safety first

A flashing check engine light means a severe misfire: slow down and stop driving soon. A red brake or oil pressure warning needs immediate attention. Pull over safely and don't keep driving.

What it means

  • The Wisconsin inspection program says a flashing light indicates a rather severe level of engine misfire.
  • Severe misfire over even a short period can seriously damage emission parts, especially the catalytic converter.
  • Mazda's manual says to park in a safe place immediately and contact a dealer, and not to drive at high speed while the light stays on or flashes.

What to do

  1. Slow down, avoid hard acceleration and heavy loads.
  2. Pull over when it's safe. Consider towing rather than driving far.
  3. Read the codes. Expect P0300–P0308 misfire codes (see our P0300 guide).
  4. Don't disconnect the battery while the light is flashing (Mazda's warning).

Sources

  1. Wisconsin Vehicle Inspection Program: OBD FAQ
  2. Mazda USA owner's manual (2024 CX-30): Check engine warning light
