Tire pressure (TPMS) light: what it means and what to do

⚡ Quick answer

A steady TPMS light usually means one or more tires are significantly underinflated. If it flashes for about a minute to a minute and a half at startup and then stays on, the TPMS itself has a fault.

🚨 Safety first

A flashing check engine light means a severe misfire: slow down and stop driving soon. A red brake or oil pressure warning needs immediate attention. Pull over safely and don't keep driving.

What it means

  • The Tire Industry Association says the light comes on when one or more tires falls below 25% under the recommended pressure.
  • Low pressure may mean the tire can't support the vehicle's load, or there's a leak that could lead to a flat or a dangerous tire failure.
  • Blinking for about 90 seconds at startup, then staying on, means the system isn't monitoring your tires at all.

What to do

  1. Check all tires with a gauge when cold and inflate to the pressure on the door-jamb placard (not the tire sidewall maximum).
  2. Look for nails, cuts or a tire that keeps losing air.
  3. If the light flashes and then stays on, have a tire professional diagnose the TPMS.

Sources

  1. Tire Industry Association: Tire Pressure Monitoring System
