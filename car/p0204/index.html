P0204: Injector Circuit/Open, Cylinder 4 (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-10-22 · Based on 2 cited source(s)

⚡ Quick answer

P0204 means the engine computer has found a fault in the electrical circuit for the cylinder 4 fuel injector. The usual cause is a bad injector, but open or shorted wiring or the engine computer (PCM) itself can also set it.

🚨 Safety first

A flashing check engine light means a severe misfire: slow down and stop driving soon. A red brake or oil pressure warning needs immediate attention. Pull over safely and don't keep driving.

What P0204 means

The powertrain control module (PCM) turns each fuel injector on and off using transistors that RepairPal calls drivers. RepairPal says that if the PCM cannot control the cylinder 4 injector, it sets P0204. OBD-Codes.com explains how it decides: when the injector is switched on the PCM expects the voltage on its control wire to be pulled low, close to zero, and when it is switched off it expects close to battery voltage. If it doesn't see the expected voltage, or the circuit resistance is excessively high or low, it sets the code. The two sources name it slightly differently: OBD-Codes.com calls it Cylinder 4 Injector Circuit Malfunction and RepairPal calls it Injector Circuit/Open, Cylinder 4, but they describe the same fault. It is a generic OBD-II code, though repair steps vary by make and model. P0201 through P0203 and P0205 through P0212 cover the other cylinders, and P0200 is the general injector circuit code.

Severity: can I keep driving?

Stop and fix promptly

Get it diagnosed soon and don't ignore it. RepairPal rates P0204 Urgent, meaning it can cause severe damage or be unsafe. OBD-Codes.com says it will likely cause a misfire, rough running and poor acceleration. If the check engine light is flashing, the engine is shaking badly, or it loses power, keep driving to a minimum and have it checked, since a misfiring cylinder can damage other parts.

Common symptoms

  • Check engine light on (OBD-Codes.com)
  • Misfire and a rough running engine (OBD-Codes.com)
  • Poor acceleration (OBD-Codes.com), or decreased engine performance (RepairPal)
  • Increased fuel consumption (RepairPal)

Likely causes

Roughly most to least common, based on the sources below. Your vehicle may differ.

  1. Bad cylinder 4 fuel injector (both sources; OBD-Codes.com says this is usually the cause)
  2. Open or short in the wiring to the injector (OBD-Codes.com), or a wiring issue (RepairPal)
  3. Bad or failed PCM (both sources)
  4. The sources agree on the list but differ in detail: OBD-Codes.com points first to the injector, while RepairPal lists injector, PCM and wiring without ranking them and names fuel injector replacement as the common repair

Diagnostic steps you can do

⚠ Before you start

Work on a cool engine, parked on level ground with the parking brake set. Never work under a car supported only by a jack.

  1. Read all stored codes. Misfire codes such as P0304 or P0300 often appear alongside P0204.
  2. Visually check the cylinder 4 injector, its connector and wiring for damage or broken wires (OBD-Codes.com).
  3. With a digital multimeter, check the injector's resistance against your vehicle's spec and replace it if it's out of spec, then check for 10 volts or more at the injector connector (OBD-Codes.com).
  4. If you can't test the injector directly, OBD-Codes.com suggests swapping it with another cylinder's injector and seeing whether the code changes, for example to P0202. If it moves, the injector is bad.
  5. Avoid grounding PCM wires unless you are certain you have the correct wire; OBD-Codes.com warns not to attempt it if you're unsure.

Typical fixes

  • Repair open or shorted wiring or a damaged connector in the cylinder 4 injector circuit (both sources list wiring)
  • Replace the cylinder 4 fuel injector if it is out of spec or the code follows it (RepairPal lists fuel injector replacement as the common repair)
  • Repair or replace the PCM only after the injector and wiring test good (both sources list the PCM as a possible cause)

Repair costs vary widely by vehicle, region, parts brand and shop, so we don't quote prices. Get a written estimate after diagnosis.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • The check engine light is flashing or the engine runs very rough
  • Testing needs an injector tester or activating the injector from the PCM driver wire, which OBD-Codes.com warns against if you're unsure of the wire
  • The injector and wiring test good and the code returns, which can point to the PCM
  • Several injector codes appear at once, which suggests a shared power feed or wiring problem rather than one injector

FAQs

Is P0204 serious?

Treat it as serious. RepairPal rates it Urgent, and OBD-Codes.com says it will likely cause a misfire and rough running. Get it diagnosed soon.

Is P0204 always a bad injector?

No. OBD-Codes.com says the injector is usually the cause, but both sources also list wiring and the PCM. Test before buying parts.

How much does it cost to fix P0204?

Neither source gives a repair cost. The price depends on whether it's wiring, the injector, or the PCM, so get a diagnosis or a written estimate before replacing parts.
