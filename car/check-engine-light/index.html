Check engine light (steady): what it means and what to do

⚡ Quick answer

A steady check engine light (also called the MIL) means the engine computer found a problem with the engine, emissions or (on some cars) transmission control system and stored a trouble code. Read the code with an OBD2 scanner.

🚨 Safety first

A flashing check engine light means a severe misfire: slow down and stop driving soon. A red brake or oil pressure warning needs immediate attention. Pull over safely and don't keep driving.

What it means

  • Mazda lists engine control, emission control and transmission control problems as triggers, and also a missing or loose fuel cap, or a very low fuel level.
  • OBD can spot emissions-related failures before you notice any drivability problem.
  • The light can go off by itself: if the OBD system checks the problem three consecutive times and doesn't see it, it turns the light off. A loose gas cap that you tighten is a common example.

What to do

  1. Check that the gas cap is tight.
  2. Read the stored codes with an OBD2 scanner and look them up in our directory.
  3. If the car drives normally, book a diagnosis soon. If it runs badly, drive gently and get it checked as soon as possible.
  4. Mazda warns: don't disconnect the battery while the light is on or flashing.

Sources
