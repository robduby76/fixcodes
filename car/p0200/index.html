P0200: Injector Circuit Malfunction (Meaning, Causes, Fixes)

Reviewed 2026-10-23 · Based on 2 cited source(s)

⚡ Quick answer

P0200 means the engine computer has found a problem in the fuel injector circuit in general, rather than pinning it on one cylinder. Causes include a shorted, open or out-of-spec injector, open, grounded or shorted driver wiring, a harness rubbing against engine parts, corroded connections, a plugged injector, or rarely the computer itself.

🚨 Safety first

A flashing check engine light means a severe misfire: slow down and stop driving soon. A red brake or oil pressure warning needs immediate attention. Pull over safely and don't keep driving.

What P0200 means

On sequential fuel injection the powertrain control module (PCM) controls each injector separately. OBD-Codes.com explains that battery voltage reaches the injectors from a fused source, usually the power distribution center, and the PCM completes each injector's ground through an internal switch called a driver. When an injector is commanded off the PCM expects high voltage on the driver circuit, and when it is commanded on it expects the voltage to be pulled low. If it doesn't see that, P0200 can set, and OBD-Codes.com notes P1222 or other injector circuit codes may set as well. RepairPal describes it as the PCM being unable to control the fuel injectors, a problem in the operation of the fuel injector circuit. The sources name it slightly differently: OBD-Codes.com calls it Injector Circuit Malfunction and RepairPal calls it Injector Circuit/Open, but they describe the same fault. It is a generic OBD-II code, though repair steps vary by make and model. P0200 is the general code; P0201 through P0212 point to a specific cylinder, so check whether one of those is stored too.

Severity: can I keep driving?

Stop and fix promptly

Get it diagnosed soon and don't ignore it. RepairPal rates P0200 Urgent, meaning it can cause severe damage or be unsafe. OBD-Codes.com says the engine may misfire at idle or highway speed, and may start and stall or not start at all. If the check engine light is flashing, the engine is shaking badly, or it stalls, keep driving to a minimum and have it checked, since stalling in traffic is a safety risk and a misfiring engine can damage other parts.

Common symptoms

  • Check engine light on (OBD-Codes.com)
  • Engine misfire and rough running or rough idle (both sources; RepairPal says only in some cases)
  • Engine may start and stall or not start at all (OBD-Codes.com)
  • Cylinder misfire codes stored alongside it (OBD-Codes.com)
  • Poor fuel economy in some cases (RepairPal)

Likely causes

Roughly most to least common, based on the sources below. Your vehicle may differ.

  1. Open or shorted fuel injector, or one whose internal resistance is out of spec even though it still works (OBD-Codes.com)
  2. Defective, plugged or dirty fuel injector (RepairPal)
  3. Open driver circuit, driver circuit shorted to ground, or driver circuit shorted to voltage (OBD-Codes.com)
  4. Wiring harness intermittently shorting against underhood components (OBD-Codes.com)
  5. Faulty or corroded injector wiring and connections (RepairPal)
  6. A shorted injector or voltage feed that blows the shared injector fuse and cuts power to all injectors (OBD-Codes.com)
  7. Bad PCM, but only after power, wiring and injectors test good (OBD-Codes.com); RepairPal warns that replacing the injector or PCM when the real problem is wiring and connections is a common misdiagnosis
  8. The sources lean differently: OBD-Codes.com focuses on electrical faults in the injector and driver circuits, while RepairPal also lists plugged or dirty injectors

Diagnostic steps you can do

⚠ Before you start

Work on a cool engine, parked on level ground with the parking brake set. Never work under a car supported only by a jack.

  1. Read all stored codes and write down the freeze frame data. A cylinder-specific code such as P0201 to P0212, or a misfire code like P0300, tells you where to look first.
  2. Check the injector fuse and look over the injector harness and connectors for chafing, loose pins, broken locks or corrosion (both sources).
  3. If you have several injector or misfire codes, OBD-Codes.com suggests unplugging all the injectors, turning the key on with the engine off, and checking for 12 volts at one wire of each connector. If power is missing at all of them, look for a blown fuse or a short to ground on the shared feed.
  4. OBD-Codes.com notes one shorted injector can take out the whole feed: replace the fuse and plug the injectors back in one at a time. If the fuse blows, the last one plugged in is shorted.
  5. With power present, a noid light plugged into each injector connector shows whether the PCM driver is pulsing. If it flashes, check that injector's resistance against your vehicle's spec (OBD-Codes.com).
  6. Injectors can fail only when hot or only when cold, so OBD-Codes.com recommends testing while the problem is happening.

Typical fixes

  • Replace a blown injector fuse and repair any short on the injector voltage feed (OBD-Codes.com)
  • Repair chafed, open, shorted or corroded wiring and connectors in the injector circuits (both sources)
  • Replace any injector that is open, shorted, out of spec, or plugged (both sources)
  • Check PCM power and grounds, and replace or repair the PCM only after everything else tests good (OBD-Codes.com; RepairPal warns this is often misdiagnosed)

Repair costs vary widely by vehicle, region, parts brand and shop, so we don't quote prices. Get a written estimate after diagnosis.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • The check engine light is flashing, the engine runs very rough, or it stalls or won't start
  • Tracing driver circuits back to the PCM needs a wiring diagram and the factory specs
  • The fuse keeps blowing or the fault comes and goes, which can mean a harness rubbing through somewhere hard to reach
  • The injectors and wiring test good and the code returns, which can point to the PCM

FAQs

Is P0200 serious?

Treat it as serious. RepairPal rates it Urgent, and OBD-Codes.com says the engine may misfire, stall or not start. Get it diagnosed soon.

What's the difference between P0200 and P0201?

P0200 is the general injector circuit code. P0201 through P0212 name a specific cylinder's injector circuit. If one of those is stored with P0200, start your checks at that cylinder.

Can one bad injector cause P0200?

Yes. OBD-Codes.com says a single shorted injector can blow the shared fuse and cut power to every injector. RepairPal also lists a defective or plugged injector as a cause.

How much does it cost to fix P0200?

Neither source gives a repair cost. The price depends on whether it's a fuse, wiring, an injector, or the PCM, so get a diagnosis or a written estimate before replacing parts.
