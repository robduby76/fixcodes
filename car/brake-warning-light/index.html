Brake system warning light: what it means and what to do

⚡ Quick answer

A red brake warning light that stays on with the parking brake released can mean low brake fluid or a brake system problem. Mazda says not to drive.

🚨 Safety first

A flashing check engine light means a severe misfire: slow down and stop driving soon. A red brake or oil pressure warning needs immediate attention. Pull over safely and don't keep driving.

What it means

  • Mazda: if the light stays on with the parking brake released, brake fluid may be low or there's a brake system problem. Driving with it on is dangerous, because the brakes may not work or could fail.
  • If both the brake and ABS lights are on, Mazda says to have the car towed.

What to do

  1. Make sure the parking brake is fully released.
  2. If the light stays on, park safely and get the brakes inspected right away. Tow if needed.

Sources

  1. Mazda USA owner's manual (2018 CX-5): Warning/indicator lights (engine oil, brake system)
