Whirlpool Oven Error Codes: What Each Code Means

Every Whirlpool oven code explained on FixCodes, 6 in all. Together they cite 26 sources (manufacturer manuals, service literature and support pages), and each guide covers what the code means, the likely causes, safe DIY checks and when to call a pro. Find the code on your display (or count the blinks) below.

CodeWhat it means
F1 E0Oven Control Memory (EEPROM) Error
F1 E0 on a Whirlpool range or wall oven is a fault inside the electronic oven control itself.
F2Temperature Sensor or Keypad, Depending on the Control
A plain F2 on a Whirlpool range or wall oven (no E number after it) usually points to the oven temperature sensor circuit.
F2 E1Stuck or Shorted Keypad
F2 E1 on a Whirlpool range or wall oven points to the keypad (touchpad).
F3 E0Oven Temperature Sensor Open
F3 E0 on a Whirlpool range or wall oven points to the oven temperature sensor circuit.
F6 E1Oven Over Temperature or Control Fault
F6 E1 (F6E1) on a Whirlpool range or wall oven means the oven control has flagged a fault in its temperature-control system.
F9Miswired Power Supply
F9 or F9 E0 (F9E0) on a Whirlpool range or wall oven means the control thinks the power coming in is wrong: most often the house outlet or the hard-wired connection to…

The same code can mean different things on different model lines. Always confirm against the manual or wiring-diagram label for your exact model number.
