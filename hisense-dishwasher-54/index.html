Hisense Dishwasher 54 (F54) Error Code: Overfilling Water, Overflow Guard Triggered

Reviewed 2026-11-06 · Based on 4 manufacturer & service sources

⚡ Quick answer

54 or F54 on a Hisense dishwasher means the overflow guard has detected too much water in the machine ("Overfilling water"). Hisense manuals say the overflow protection starts pumping the water out and shuts off the water supply when the level goes above normal. Turn the water tap (supply valve) off first, then call Hisense service. Do not unplug the dishwasher until the tap is off, or water can leak onto the floor. This F-code table is used on models such as HS673C60WUK, HV643D90 and HUI6220XCUS; older E-code Hisense dishwashers use E4 or E5 for overflow instead.

What the code means

On Hisense dishwashers that show F-codes (for example the UK HS673C60WUK, HV643D90 / HV643D90UK and the US HUI6220XCUS), F54 means Overfilling water. The HS673C60WUK manual's overflow guard section says that if the water level exceeds normal, the guard starts pumping the machine out and turns off the water supply, and F54 (Overfilling water) appears on the display. The troubleshooting table repeats F54 as Overfilling water with the action: turn off the water tap and contact your nearest service centre, and warns not to remove the plug from the wall socket until the water tap on the supply line has been turned off. The HV643D90 manual uses the same F54 Overfilling water wording and adds that after F54 the dishwasher can only be turned off once the error is cleared (other codes clear with a button press). The US HUI6220XCUS operating instructions list F54 as Overfilling and say to turn off the water supply valve and call the Customer Care Center; that manual's overflow guard section matches the same idea (pump out and shut off the supply when the level is too high). F10 is also labelled Overfilling water on these tables, but its first action is to clear the drainage pump; treat F10 and F54 as related overflow codes with different first steps. Older Hisense dishwashers that use E1/E2/E3-style codes (for example HSCE14FS) list overflow as E4, not 54 — check the table in your own manual.

Likely causes

  • The overflow / flood guard has detected a water level above normal (the cause Hisense names for F54)
  • A leaking or stuck-open water inlet valve letting water in when it shouldn't (a common overflow cause; Hisense names a related F40 inlet valve leakage code that also says turn off the tap and call service)
  • A blocked filter or drain path so the machine can't pump out as fast as it fills (F10 Overfilling water first asks you to clear the drainage pump; worth checking before you assume a parts failure)
  • A faulty water-level sensor or pressure switch reporting a false overfill (likely technician cause once the tap is off and there's no real flood)

Safe DIY checks

⚠ Before you start

Unplug the appliance (or switch off its breaker) and shut off the water supply valves before removing panels or disconnecting hoses. Keep towels handy for residual water.

  1. Turn off the water tap (supply valve) feeding the dishwasher first. Hisense says do this before you unplug the machine so water doesn't keep flowing onto the floor.
  2. Once the tap is off, you can unplug the dishwasher or switch it off at the socket. Look under and around the machine for standing water; mop up any spill.
  3. If the tub still has water above the filters, with power and water off, lift out the coarse and fine filters, scoop out as much water as you can safely reach, and check the drain pump area for a stuck object (coins, glass, plastic) — the same "clear the drainage pump" steps Hisense gives for the related F10 overfill code.
  4. Check the inlet hose connection and the area under the door for drips that point to a leak, then leave the machine off until a technician has checked it.
  5. Do not keep restarting programmes while F54 is active. On the HV643D90 Hisense says the machine can only be turned off after F54 once the error is cleared, and the official action is still to turn the tap off and call service.

🛠 When to call a pro

  • F54 keeps coming back or the machine fills when it shouldn't: the inlet valve, level sensor or control board needs testing with the water supply isolated.
  • There's water in the base pan, a whirring drain pump that won't stop, or water on the floor: leave the tap off and call Hisense / a qualified technician. The US HUI6220XCUS manual says a continuous whirring drain pump after overflow protection means shut off the water, unplug, and call Customer Care.
  • The dishwasher is under warranty: contact Hisense customer service rather than replacing parts yourself.
